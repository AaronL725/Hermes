Money Management There are so many guides out there on how to properly manage money, the options are limitless on what you do. As for me heres how i manage mine. First off, my stop loss is always 50 pips. ALWAYS. not one pip lower or one pip higher. My take profit is not 2:1 ratio or 3:1 ratio anymore. My take profit is as price action develops. In a ranging market if i scalp i may take anything from 5 pips to 50 pips. In a trending market i may keep a position for as long as 150 pips. If a position is getting close to my target where i know strong support or resistance will be reached, I may choose to get out of the position entirely or at least close half of it. The way I see things, Its best to get out and protect gains made when in doubt then to let the market bully you around when your not sure of direction. There is always another entry waiting around the corner, no rush. Secondly, there are 2 kinds of trades that I place. the first kind is where i am pretty confident we have hit a strong resistance or support and that there is limited room to be stopped out. This position is regular size. Then there is the second kind of order where I am not so confident anymore but I am pretty sure of direction. If we reach a temporary resistance where PA is struggling to pass, I may place a sell order which is equal to 1/3rd of a normal position. If I am correct, I will make a decent profit and have not lost the opportunity to make some quick pips. If I get stopped out, I have lost only 20pips of a regular position size but gained a much better entry point for where I feel confident in placing a regular size position. It is really a win win situation that I have done many times with limited downside risk as long as analysis of the PA is correct. Thirdly and last, position size. Position size is relative to account size. A simple rule of thumb, for every 2000dollars in account, pip size increases by 1dollar. So if at 1000 dollars you do 1dollar pips, at 2000 you do 2dollar pips but then you wont do 3 dollar pips until 4000 and 4 dollar pips until 6000. Do not ask me for how i would do things past 10000 as i have never been there. I am 22 after all and have started with little money and am trying to accomplish the highly improbably, rags to riches. Sidenote: forgot to mention, while the stop loss is 50pips always, I always try to look for trades that have the potential for at least 50pips, even in scalping conditions

Trading strategy (about time right) My strategy is K.I.S.S (keep it stupid simple). I use no indicators. I repeat, I use no indicators which is why this strategy is not going to work for the majority. I am my own indicator and I determine what the trend is through what the eyes see and the mind interprets. The challenge is interpreting things correctly. Countless hours watching price action has developed this ability and in a sense I am my own indicator. If you think you can do this, then this is the right thread for you, otherwise, I recommend the THV thread by cobraforex. There are a few other good threads out there, you just have to look. I use mainly supports and resistances. Price action does not move in a straight line. It moves up and down and left to right, always. Price action is 2dimensional. If 1.2850 is strong resistance and 1.2750 is strong support, we will not go from the resistance to the support in an instant (with the exception of news releases), we go in channels. Price action is always contained within a range. (I will go into detail on this in a later post with charts). I also use patterns. For the EUR/USD there are a few main patterns that develop all the time. W's and M's, h&amp;s, cup and handles, triangles (the most common pattern), flags and flagpoles. There may be others but this is all I am interested in. I use this in combination with what i perceive as strong support and resistance as well as the upper or lower wall of a channel (rarely the middle wall). News. I am always aware of the news. Trichet said this, feds cut rate to that, oil spiked up because of this or that, new president this, holiday that. I am always aware of the news. However I rarely trade the news. This is because with so many news out there, its hard to know which news will impact the markets, especially these days. The one exception of trading the news is when its a clear cut case of "buy the rumor, sell the news". This happens clearly when the market trends in a direction and sticks at that low or high for up to several days before a news release at which point we see a quick reversal in the other direction after the news release. Trading fundies 101, simple and clear cut (most of the time haha), any other scenario is too big of a gamble. Starting with my next post I will explain through charts how i do what i do exactly.

Before anything else I take a birds eye view of what is going on with price action. Channels are everywhere from the largest timeframe to the lowest timeframe. The birds eye view is very simple, just take a look at the bigger timeframes and draw the channel walls to see where price action is contained. To make the upper wall, simply connect the highest spikes. To make the lower wall, simply connect the lowest spikes. To make the middle wall, simply draw a line through the middle where the largest amount of spikes are visible as that point divides bear and bullish sentiment within the trend. Similar to andrews pitchfork however i find my way of doing this is alot more accurate Attached Image (click to enlarge)

--- 帖子 #10 ---
When a channel is fully developed and interpreted correctly there are 4 main things that i look for when deciding entries. One is, what is pa doing, is it sticking to the lower wall or the upper wall. This is crucial because when pa stick to one wall for an extended period of time, it will most likely break hard to the other wall in one big move with the middle wall acting as first support or resistance (which is why i may choose to partially close position or fully close position at the middle wall). Secondly is, wall extensions. Each time a wall is tapped or close to being tapped there is a potential breakout coming. How do we know whether a wall will be broken or extended? depends on the timeframe you are looking at. If a channel is drawn on the 15min timeframe, any candlestick that exits the upper or lower wall is not the deciding factor. The deciding factor is where a candlestick closes. If it closes within the channel with the spike outside the wall, you know that was just an extension. If the 15min candlestick closes outside the wall, the very next candlestick has to close within the wall for it to be an extension. If that second candle closes outside the walls, you can consider it a temporary breakout if price action does not return within the channel walls. Sometimes when breakout occurs, price just hangs there with no real strength to go any further and most times returns within the channel (as no indicators are used, this si where gut feeling comes in and judging the strength of bulls or bears). Thirdly, I look for any patterns that may develop within that channel that may indicate future direction. A bull flag indicates continuation up, an M indicates a potential reversal and breakout and so on( again this will come easy with lots of hours spent watching pa). THe last main thing that I look for when Judging price action within a channel is the relative strength of the trend. The longer the candlesticks in a particular direction, the stronger the trend, the more candlesticks that are bundled together within a small range, the more I know the strength of the trend is either coming to a stop or is facing strong resistance/support before continuation(interpreting this correctly again comes with experience. Breakouts. Lets say you are in a trade, you buy at the lower wall in an uptrend yet breakout occurs and you get stopped out. What a tragedy!..you just lost some money. Wrong. No strategy is guaranteed 100% success. You will make more money playing with the trend then gambling on a potential breakout. I speak from experience. The trend is your friend, the triend is your friend. So, breakout occurs, price action exits the channel and you are looking for your next entry. Oh what to do what to do, how to decide. There are 2 ways here. One which I use alot is wait for the retest of the breakout wall. IF the break is down, the upper wall gets retest to make sure it has become resistance at which point you may enter the trend short as well. The second method is just simply patiently wait. Price action always moves in channels. Once one trend ends, another develops..simple as that. Once the new trend develops clearly, you can use the same entry criteria as before, upper wall or lower wall always being aware of potential patterns that could form, strength of bulls and bears and so on.
