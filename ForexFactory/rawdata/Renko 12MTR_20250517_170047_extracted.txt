okay here goes. tested a few, and this is from a system that I used to follow but it required constant monitoring so i stopped. Bascially, its ADX levels. Adx shows the momentum of the trade. (IT DOES NOT TELL YOU TO SELL OR BUY. Rather, it tells you WHEN its a good time to enter a trade) Settings 5 levels. 7-Green -fixed min 0 Fixed max 100 Levels 30 and 50 18- Blue- uncheck the boxes and no levels 42- Orange- uncheck the boxes and no levels 89- Pink- uncheck the boxes and no levels 144-White- uncheck the boxes and no levels +DI -DI None for both. Im gonna use a normal chart first, then later show it on the renko charts. Can be hard to grasp at first. but its easy as you get used to it. Okay. the first thing you look for is the compression of all 5 levels. In the first screen shot, the red line. its all compressed together. This means the market is probably ranging or the trade has lost power. What it also tells you is, its powering up the next move. Rmb- it shows the momentum of the trade. What we want, ideally is the Fan effect. as shown by the blue line. Shortly after, we can see a 20 pip drop. Another example is the second screenshot, compression at red line, fan effect at blue line. What we want is all 5 adx levels to be up to show that a trade has strong momentum. So basically. when its long and flat, there's no power. when its compressing, its gearing up for a big move.

Not sure you understand how to set up a Renko chart. That is very hard to explain From another thread see if this helps. Download Renko Chart Builder Indicator or EA to your temporary folder outside drive C:, Scan it for virus protection If you use Windows 7 or above, make sure your MT4 program folder is outside of your Windows / Program files. Copy or move your downloaded Renko Chart Builder program into MT4 folder. if this is Indicator type copy to MT4 Expert/Indicator folder, and if this is EA type then copy to MT4 Expert folder, Restart your MT4 if necessary. Open your MT4 platform/program, and open M1 standard candlestick chart of pair that you need to create your offline renko chart as source data chart, for example M1 EURUSD. Release auto scroll if active, and press Home Button at your Keyboard, to make sure you get enough bars for your source data chart. (for normal use, 2 or 3 months M1 bars data is enough). Open your navigator window box, search your Renko Chart Builder Name. for Indicator type, search in custom indicator folder, for EA type, search in expert folder. Click and Drag to your M1 source data chart, if window box input setting not automatically show up, then right click on your source data chart and select Indicator list ( Ctrl + I ) for Indicator type of Renko Chart Builder, or press F7 for EA type of Renko Chart Builder to open input setting window box. Adjust the input setting, as you need, for this example of EURUSD. and suppose you use my MTH Composite Currency Renko Chart Builder Indi v01. just follow my explanation about input setting for this indicator above. At Top Left Corner of your MT4 Platform, Select 'File' Menu and Select 'Open Offline' menu to open offline chart window box. For this example, search for EURUSD, M10 and double click it to open your EURUSD Offline Renko Chart. Apply your indicators or template to your offline renko chart. I wish you success with your trading. =================================== =====================
