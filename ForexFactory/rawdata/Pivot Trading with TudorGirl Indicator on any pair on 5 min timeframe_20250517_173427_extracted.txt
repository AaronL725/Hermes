Hi HT, This is an excellent question. The three indicators are a complementary suite. Let me explain. Indicators are all price filters. They can either: (a) INTEGRATE - like a MA etc. These act as smoothers but introduce lag, OR (b) DIFFERENTIATE - like momentum. These introduce noise but have slightly leading properties. All indicators fall into either one class or another. It is ABSOLUTELY POINTLESS to put more than one of each sort on a chart - they will just tell you the same thing. You see charts loaded up with MA, MACD, RSI etc and they all tell you the SAME THING!! When Edge says that you only ever need two indicators, tirgger and confirmation, he is absolutely right - the mathematics supports his statement. That is why the indicators were written as they are. On the other thread, folk have tried adding VQ, QQE, Forex Scalping, etc and then abandoned them when they realied that the TudorGirl suite already tells you EVERYTHING you need to know. Paulus, Edge and several other experienced traders have said this on many occasions but no-one is listening!! The TudorGirl suite has: (1) TrendMagic - for determining MOVING STOPS , (2) HG_0001a - a combined INTEGRATE/DIFFERENTIATE indy for ENTRIES . There is also HG_0001a_MTF for those who want to see the same thing on multiple time frames. NOTE: Remember that the MTF indy can repaint (they all can due to the way MT handles them internally) but the STF DOES NOT REPAINT . (3) Hydrodynamic Vorticity - a measure of turbulence to help with EXITS . Paulus Classic setup uses TrendMagic and HG_0001a_MTF. Edge has posted charts using HG_0001a and Vorticity. Nisha is now suggesting HG_0001a and Pivots. So.... You can use them how you like! But if you understand how they work individually, you will see how they can work together. Anne
