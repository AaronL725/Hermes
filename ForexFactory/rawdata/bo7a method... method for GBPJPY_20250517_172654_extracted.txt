If you exit with a profit, you can re-enter trend after 2 things happen: 1. price retests 7 ema and bounces back 2. price then makes new high or low for the current move After price has retested, you can set entry order just below or above the low or high point for the current move. For counter trend crosses, defined as crosses going up with price below 90 or crosses going down with price above the 90, you can take the cross but I would tighten up the stop and limit. Don't tighten stop closer than 55 or 50, though. I would suggest 55 stop and 90 or a 100 limit on these counter trend crosses. And just as with trend following crosses, do not take if 90 is sideways.

This is just an add on to make things a little easier. Great for alerts. Here Goes: The three EMAs, 7, 15, and 90 are represented by the blue, yellow and magenta lines on the chart. Same colors for the three CCIs in the subwindow. Now for the rules. For Longs: Rule 1- PA should Above EMA90 Rule 2- PAabove EMA7 and 7EMA above 15EMA. Rule 3- Entry - Wait for PA to retrace back to 7 EMA then enter provided Rule 1 and 2 are intact. Once filled, Set SL and TP , specially for US accounts. In the subwindow are three lines of the same color as the EMA. If the the Magenta and the Yellow are above zero, and the blue is below zero, get ready for a trade. That is the retraces of 7 ema rule 3 for entry. Enter Long when it crossed zero from below. Reverse for Shorts. Yellow and Magenta below zero. Blues crosses zero from above. to enter short. The blue vertical lines meet all rules for Long. Red - short. Good part about the CCI indis is the cross of the =/-100 lines. If the Magenta is above 100 , yellow is above zero and the blue crosses zero from below, the probability of trade being profitable increases. The simplest way to trade this indicator is if all lines are above zero and increasing in value, go long- reverse for short. In the second chart the ema values are changed to 7, 50 and 200. Which will yield lesser trades but more profitable, 200 and 50 emas are used by a whole lot of people. Anytime an EMA is used, the cci of same period will offer additional insights of the interaction between the the PA and the EMA. Read up on Baby Pips and wiki.
