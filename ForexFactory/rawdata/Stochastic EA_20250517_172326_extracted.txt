So loses will be calculated based on init size which is very small * SL. on the other hand profit to be based on greater size "multiply of init size" 6- Keep the lose pos, and let the size do the job. I am trading in this way for a long time, manually. I think it made a good result. But it is not easy to manage it manually, and almost all failed trades are upon to my execution mistakes in manual trading.
