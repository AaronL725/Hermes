I am connecting 2 charts showing some signals: The first chart is the 1H / 4H Gold chart witch I love trading. I have all the signals down so the idea will be clear as to what is a signal for me. What is a signal: First we look at the higher time frame, here the 4H, a signal here is NOT a cross of the GREEN and RED lines, it when the GREEN and RED are on the OPPOSITE side of the BLUE LINE, THE 1.00. Then I go to the shorter time frame witch in this occasion is the 1H, and I take only the signals that correspond with the higher time frame, here the 4H, again only the ones that are crossing the blue. Of course if you took the first signal you do not take the second, I just market all the signal in the right direction. Now the second chart is the chart of a 15min / 1H of the GBPUSD with the same idea. For areas where I might have placed my stops I have the x. Also if I am in profit and one of the lines green or red cross the blue on the other side I will close the position and wait for another signal. Let's do not forget that when both lines green and red are on the same side of the blue line there is no signal, they have to be on the opposite. Let's do not forget that I use this as a tool and we will go deeper in this together.
