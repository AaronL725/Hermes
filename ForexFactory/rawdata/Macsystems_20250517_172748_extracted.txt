For the moving average settings, use a cross of 10 and 50. the idea is to wait for the Moving averages to compress... meaning market is waiting for a move. the move will surely happen if one is PATIENT. it can happen in Asian, Europe or American session. Once the market moves, it will take some time to settle and the process repeats itself again. Doesn't work like clockwork hence the need to WAIT for it to happen by itself. Think of the market like a rubber band.... if you stretch it by any means (fundamental news like brexit) or something else, it will find time to relax. If the market relaxes so much for a day or two, it will expand and move to another level. Its never static. Having the discipline to DO NOTHING in between is key no matter the news out there. The idea is to wait for the market to "compress itself" and trade in the direction of the following BREAKOUT. Normally, a breakout will happen to signify movement away from the compression. Note: there can be fake movements, hence i prefer a MTF approach to see whats happening in the higher TF up to 1hr when trading the 15m or 30m charts. I prefer to 1. SELL after a compression breakout under the 4hr supertrend line 2. BUY after a compression breakout above the 4hr supertrend line Once the breakout happens, watch out for the first DIAMOND on the BBMACD indicator: green for BUY and red for SELL. The daily pivots provide reasonable profit targets. you can equally draw S/R lines on the 4hr and see where to take profits. you can allow you entry to run till market relaxes after the BO. Does it work all the time? NO. nothing works 100% of the time in the FX world hence a good MM is required. Know when news will happen and trade accordingly. my observation is that a success rate of 60-70% is achievable and i will working towards that. Again, cherry picking the best set ups will guarantee up to 90% success when executed accordingly.

I use levels/ recent high or low as my SL while aiming for key support/ resistance or 00 levels for TP.
