This trade is a real time trade. It happens at the moment that the NLD, the Super Trend, and the Super_Trend, all become the same color. When this happens, it is time to enter the trade. The best way to know when this happens is to have the NLD alerts on, and you will get a notification alert. When you get that alert, you can come to your chart and see if the other two indicators line up. If they do, enter the market, and if they do not, wait a bit until they do, and then enter the trade. This little piece of confirmation is what was missing before. Just the NLD by itself was good, but not enough to rule out the false signals. The addition of these two other simple indicators helps rule out the false trades. Nothing has changed from what I originally introduced, except I have added confirmation that the NLD was better likely not to be a false signal.
