I'm sure someone here will pick this up I decided to share this strategy to give something back, maybe help beginners with something simple, and, hopefully, if it makes money we could all do something good and help feed more families (more about this later on). Step-By-Step Goal: - 50pips/day - Get out of the market as soon as possible. TradingView - I'm using TradingView because I like the specific PSAR indicator with Buy/Sell labels.
