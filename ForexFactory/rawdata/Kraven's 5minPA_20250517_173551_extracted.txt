I’m not claiming this is original, the holy grail or anything, however if It has been done before I haven’t see it. OK, I’ll try to keep this brief and clear. The strategy is a simple PA one based around physiological numbers. 5min chart Indi's: momentum, signal bar, sweet spots Any pair (although I only use GBP/USD and EUR/USD) SELLING: When the price is moving DOWN approaching a round number... let’s say 1.5100 (5100, 4200, 3700 etc...) note the momentum indi (Is it down and moving downwards, under 100) note Signal Bars are they all RED. If so wait until price passes 1.500 by 5pips or so then enter 4 SELL lots with a SL of 25. First TG will be 1.475 (25pips) we then move the other 3 lots to BE SL, TG2 is 1.450 (50pips) move SL to 1.475, TG3 is 1.425 (75pips) SL moved to 1.450. Final TG is 1.400 (100+pips) or just let it run, SL 1.425. So basically we target 25pips each lot and protect profit by moving our SL 25pips each time. Opposite for BUYING, momentum above 100 and going up (hopefully), Signal Bar all GREEN. Ideally I would love someone to create an EA of this, should be simple for a programming mastermind. DisplayTheMas = true will display the Ma's on the chart AlertOn will use the Mt4 Alert system for cross over notifications SoundOn plays a sound when a cross over occurs ArrowsOn places arrows on the chart when a crossover occurs. Play around with the settings to get what you are looking for...
