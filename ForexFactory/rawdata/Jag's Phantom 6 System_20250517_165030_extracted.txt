Following is an excerpt regarding a working EA. It may or may not provide programmers with some ideas. The EA, in some ways works like the Phantom EA's but only trades 2 pairs and is not a hedging strategy. This info is either useful or useless. Make of it what you will. “The EA trades two currency pairs. Based on the frequency setting, the EA checks to see if the conditions to open an order are met. It does that for both the currency pairs used for trading. If there are no previous orders, it opens one in the direction suggested by the trend algorithm. If there are already other open orders, and if the distance from the last open order is greater than the number of pips set in the order_step setting, it opens a new order in the same direction with the same lot size. As soon as the profit for all the open orders for each pair is equal to or greater than the target_profit it it closes all the open orders for that pair. On average, around 2 trades per day are opened and closed. But you can usually expect between 0 up to 4 trades a day with the default frequency setting (4 = every 12 hours). If the total $ value of all the open orders (both currency pairs) is in loss of more than the max_total_DD percentage value, all the orders are closed. This is a “safety net” in case of a huge, sudden change in trend. This has already happened during the demo and live testing period but the max_total_DD has never been reached, thanks to the fact that the lot size used for each order was small enough. The EA also recovers from these situations quickly, usually in a matter of a few days. In the counter-trend phases it is crucial to have enough margin to withstand even the largest moves. For example, during the big drop during the first few days of May 2010, the EA suffered a temporary draw down of around 20% and it didn't even touch our initial investment as it was the 3rd month of trading and we were already in profit of about 40%. Note that the profit check and draw down check are made in real time. The frequency setting determines only how often the EA will check the market for conditions to open new trades. Strategy Settings initial_balance (1000) This is the balance that the EA will consider as its only available amount for trading. Many of the money management settings depend on this value. If you have an account balance of $1,000 you should leave it at the default setting of 0.01 lots (10 cents per pip m/l). If you have $2,000 you can change it to 0.02. For $10,000 you can change it to 0.1 lots. It is strongly suggested that you use this lot size (or less) as it is the right balance between risk and performance. (TR note; this is a nice feature. For example, if you have a 5k account but only want the EA to trade with 2k.......). risk_percent if you want to have the lot size automatically adjusted set this value to (suggested) 1. The frequency setting is probably the most important for the EA strategy. It is strongly suggested that you leave it at the default value (4). Setting values below 4 will make the EA open orders more frequently. They may increase profits but may also increase risk. Based on our back tests and forward tests, the setting of 4 has proved to be the best balance between performance and risk. Using a more conservative setting (5) will instruct the EA to only open orders once a day which can lead to lower performance but also lower risks. max_total_DD (30) This percentage is the maximum loss of the actual balance that the EA can stand before closing all open orders for all pairs. " peace/out TR

You will get the Open first then the High then the Low then the Close... since each pair might be in different states in terms of PL at each of the four ticks then it will only close once the entire PL is over your threshold ($100 for example). This only happens at one of the 4 ticks per bar. So if I choose to use 30 minute OHLC for very fast testing it will check 4 times each 30 minutes in the history to see if my profit threshold was met. At the high of my EURUSD long it will also compare that with the High of my GBPUSD short... so it probably won't close because my PL is most likely not $100... but at the Close it will check again and it may have jumped over $100 and to something like $150. There's nothing stopping you from trading this way... in fact it may be better to use this because if your EA is actually trading with the trend then chances are you will get into a situation where you are in profit bigtime instead of taking your small 1% cut you will probably get more.
