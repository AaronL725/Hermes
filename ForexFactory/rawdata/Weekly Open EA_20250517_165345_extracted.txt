Rules: Week's open price is the starting point EA places 3 pending long positions 50 points above EA places 3 pending short positions 50 points below For each of these sets of pending orders... 1st order: TP 25 / SL 50 2nd order: TP 50 / SL 50 3rd order: TP None / SL 50 Long example: Pending long positions are triggered Pending short orders are moved up 50 pips to the week's open price If the long positions hit 50 (also closing trade 1 &amp; 2) then the 3rd order is set to break even on the stop loss and the short positions are moved up another 50 pips (now 50 above week's open price) Short positions will be moved up 50 each time another 50 is gained on the long trades (my defaults but can be changed). The 3rd long trade also moves it's stop loss up by 50 pips at a time (i.e. 100+ then stop loss locks in 50 and so on to the extent of where the Short orders follow).
