I follow sometimes this thread as pivot points are a must whenever I go intraday. Allow me (witha all respect to nomask) to clarify some stuff. In my experience the most challenging thing about pivot points - S/R lines is the entry itself whenever the market is reaching a level. I have some suggestions for all of you As the market normally fades (retraces) when it get (sometimes closer) to any significant level, you can go market as nomask suggest or have a pre-set (stop order) resting position, around 4-5 ticks (in case of forex) away from the level. The advantages of going in with a market order on a candlestick formation is that you have certain level of assurance, besides a common behavior on the markets at the level. But a common mistake I've seen is the temptation of pulling the trigger before the candle closes. Another downfall is a bad fill due slippage/broker manipulation etc. Pulling the trigger (market order ) for some traders is a real pain as you tend to mess things up once you see the first sign of heat against your position. I place resting orders against pivots S/R lines always....all the time. The risk is the same. Worst case scenario will be the market blasting off the pivot (breaking out) Whenever this happens, and I get stopped out I simply look to fade the other side of the pivot, no sweat. If you could add to the mix some trailing stop settings @ 1 tick per move, that will definitely increase your odds. Peace
