Entry rules: BUY: If a bearish Renko candle intersects the indicator's lower band or closes below the lower band , and the next Renko brick is bullish , open a BUY position, set the stoploss level three bricks plus spread away from the entry point. SELL: If a bullish Renko candle intersects the indicator's upper band or closes above the upper band , and the next Renko brick is bearish , open a SELL position, set the stoploss level three bricks plus spread away from the entry point. Exit Options: 1. If the position is in profit, exit after the first reverse brick. 2. If price has touched the opposite band, close the position. 3. Dear Client, To use the Period Converter to create charts from M1 history data, follow these steps. 1. Download the Period converter a. http://codebase.mql4.com/276 2. Place the converter in the ‘scripts’ folder under the data folder of the FinFX MetaTrader 4 platform a. Most commonly it is something like ‘File -&gt; Open an Data: MQL4- &gt; Scripts ’ 3. Open a chart of the currency pair you wish to use. For example EURUSD with 1 minute (M1) periodicity. 4. Drag the period_converter on the chart from the ‘Scripts’ folder of 'Navigator' window 5. On 'Common' tab check 'Allow DLL imports' and clear 'Confirm DLL function calls' checkbox. 6. On 'Inputs' properties tab please set 'ExtPeriodMultiplier' variable value. a. Example1: from M1 chart you can make a M5 chart with multiplier 5. b. Example2: from H1 chart you can make a H4 chart with multiplier 4. 7. Click OK 8. Open the created chart in offline mode ('File – Open Offline'). The chart will be updated every 2 sec (by default) while original chart with attached 'Period_converter.mq4' is open. Strategy Tester tip: Export chart data from the client terminal then import it to a symbol you want to test. Then launch testing.
