Hi everyone, wanted to post this simple way of making pips and find out if anyone can show there most simple setups for us all to share... This is based on trading trend line breaks, none of the subjective TLs that seem to get placed almost all over peoples charts but a short term TL drawn on the 1H-15min time frames.... heres the basics.... 1/ There is a strong move in price, preferably with a trend already in place and in that direction, but not a requirement. 2/ There is a short consolidation/retracement which we can draw a trend line under 3/ trade the break. How to trade this will depend on you but it is possible to use a stop loss of 15pips for most pairs or you can also use the high of the retrace. how you consider the TL broken is also up to you but even a 5-10 pip break is good in most cases. You can also wait for the 15min bar to close but doing this will loose a lot of the move in a lot of the cases. Quite often we will get some kind of PA trigger bar at the top/bottom of the retracement which can be traded in the conventional way. The first target or problem area will,in most cases, be the low of the retracement, but of course price can run much further if we catch a trend. This is a great way to add to positions also if you are already in a trade. AND before you start i consider the TL valid after only 2 touches for this setup. Warning signs that it is not going to be a good trade are when price meanders along for too long. I like the retracement to be short and sweet capturing the momentum of the previous move, preferably up to a well defined R/S level before showing obvious signs of exhausting. oh...and i personally prefere a sell off to a long as there seems to be a quicker, stronger move. have fun... jon

Hi Jon Really like the look of this method of trading as it is simple and appears to work well on all time scales. As I said earlier I have only recently discovered this method so haven't actively traded it live yet. However from my little research so far I think the secret to trading this method successfully is: Trade a strong move in the direction of the overall trend. Positioning the trendline correctly to maximise the chance of a good entry Relatively small stops so you can get at least 1:2 risk reward ratio Letting the trade run to its maximium by using trendlines again to exit. Heres an example of what I mean from this mornings London session, assuming the charts post ok. First I like to use a pair that is trending strongly like EURCHF. To me I use a 200ema and in the case of EURCHF like to see the price under the 200ema on all timeframes but in particular the shorter ones from 1 hour down.(see chart attached "Which pair today" 1hr chart. Having decided EURCHF is a good pair to trade,looking for a sell setup, I put a trendline on the 5 min chart and wait for a break. Once the break occurrs as at 0600hrs this morning then I drop down to the 1 min chart looking for the first pullback for entry. The secret is to wait for a nice clean pullback, this one was an excellent example as used 200ema as resistance, could of been pulled into trade early by move down on 06.37 candle but it wouldn't of mattered if you had as 10 pip stop would of covered it ok. Another way to aviod false breaks is to wait until you have 3 good touches on trendline as confirmation of trendline then enter on break of that 3 touch trendline. Sometimes of course the trade will go without you but that's trading! Once in trade get to break even after 4/5 pips, because I am expecting this type of trade to move away sharply from my stop. If it comes back I want out at break even and will try to re-enter again if I still feel the setup is good. On 1 min charts I think it is perfectly reasonable to have a couple of goes at re-entries especially on these trades as the risk reward is worth going for. If you get the trendlines right these trades should quickly drop away from entry. If that doesn't happen get out at B/E asap as the pullback hasn't finished! 1st entry was at 1.1673. Once in trade and stop at break even then put another trendline down the trade, what I call my "trade trendline" and hold the trade until the price breaks that trendline as it did here at 1.1631 for a 42 pip win. Adjust these "trade trendlines" as the price goes down by moving it to the last 2 significant pionts of resistance. A significant piont is a candle which has 2 candles either side lower than its high. This is illustrated better on the 2nd trade where you can see I have moved the trendline down as these significant pionts appear. Exit when a candle opens the other side of the trendline. 2nd trade entry at 1.1630 and exit at 1.1562 for 68 pips. From my back testing you can quite often get these trades at London and New York open when the price is moving fast. Not often you get 2 trades like this as quite often the first pullback is further down the move. however very similar trade happened on Friday afternoon too at the New York open. As putting the trendlines on correctly is so important to this type of trading style I have attached a pdf file I found on another thread which describes how to place trendlines. I found it very useful especially the bit about starting trendlines from right to left not left to right! Invaluable! Not so keen on th targeting method described but others might find it useful. File attached. Thanks again Jon for starting this thread I think this is definately the way to go when trading Forex. All the best Clive

I just want to clear up a bit of stuff...... First, why Price Pivot Zones (PPZs) are important to me. There is plenty of advice out there about finding the PPZs, especially on the Great J16 thread so I won’t go into it here, except to say I draw in my own and do not use floor pivots. · Number one for me is simply to find the important areas on the chart. Areas where the orders are likely to be stacked up. These are the areas where we can expect price to react strongest. It is these orders that can turn price on a dime, and if that is going to happen we need to know about it. If price is running into one of these areas and the order stack (OS) is large enough it will reverse price in a breath. Now that reversal does not want to be confused for the type of short term retracement I am looking for, I am looking to trade a continuation of a previous move. I don’t want to be left guessing whether or not price is reversing or consolidating. I just want an excuse to get in a move and trade some pips while the momentum is strong. This why it is important to only take the shorter retracement moves and why I don’t personally like the long dragged out affairs. Don’t get me wrong price might continue just the same but I personally think it becomes less and less likely to get a strong move as time goes on. · Secondly I want to know if price has just moved through a PPZ. If it has we can assume it has eaten the OS around that area. If this is so we can expect some kind of retracement as they run out and the smart money is looking for value bid/asks. Let’s assume we get a big drop in price and it dropped right through a good PPZ, we can expect to see the bears still in control of the market, that means to me that I can have more confidence that price is simply retracing (usually back to the PPZ....time and time again!) and not reversing or stalling. Again It may well be, but imho i think it’s a bigger probability that the retrace will result in another move down. Not only that but we now have a clear price that we can plan to trade from. Now imagine if price dropped into a PPZ but didn’t close below, what do we get? Well if we are lucky we might get a Pin Bar showing signs that price is reversing and the bears have lost control and the downward momentum I want to trade is gone. · Lastly I want to know if price is between PPZs. As we know price tends to move in the direction of least resistance so that means there probably won’t be enough orders coming on the market (obviously there will be times, news etc) to change the direction of the trend until price reaches a PPZ. So now I know if price gives a small retracement it is probable it will continue further after the retracement, giving me a good trade opportunity if I can catch the break of the retracement. jon
