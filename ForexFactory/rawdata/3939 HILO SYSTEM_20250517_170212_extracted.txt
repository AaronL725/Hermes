--- 帖子 #48 ---
When the EA is set, there is one, and only one thing at this time that I will change. the "Short only, Long only, Long and Short". Everyone has there favorite indicators to determine trend. I recommend using them. When you get an idea of which way its trending, set the Long, or Short only parameter. Now, the way the system works. The Candlesticks will move in and outside the 39hi/39lo channel. There will be moments where a Candlestick will start outside the channel. "MEANING, THE CANDLESTICK OPENED OUTSIDE THE CHANNEL." when that happens, this system is made to open a trade at that very moment. Now, if the Candlestick opens above the channel, the it will enter a Long trade. If the Candlestick opens below the channel, then a Short trade will be opened. Now, in terms of max trades, lets assume that the trend is in a Short trend, and the next candlestick that opens, opens below the channel, then a short trade will open, then most likely the next candle will be below the channel again, then another Short position will open. It will open one more Short trade on the next candlestick opening below the channel, so long as a Long trade has not opened up. Just remember a maximum of 5 trades. So, 2 Long trades can still open up, but only if the opportunity presents itself. Exit Strategy: Simple, 80SL, 100TS, If it reverses on you, you take an 80 pip stop loss. If the trades go in your favor, then it will eventually go into the 100 trailing stop. This is where the success "IMO", comes from. The GBP/JPY, is very volatile, making this method successful.

JR, ADX is a great trend indicator. However, you must keep in mind that it is working a little differently here on the forex side. In equities and futures, ADX is reporting the actual cumulated amount of orders in a given bar. In forex ADX is only reporting the number of trades. It doesn't know if it was 1000 1 contract trades, or 1000 5 contract trades, or 1000 .2 contract trades. You are only seeing that there was activity. You don't get the full monty.
