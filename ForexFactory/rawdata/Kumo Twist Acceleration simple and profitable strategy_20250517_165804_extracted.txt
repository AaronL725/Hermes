Hi, I agree that the way it's been described is almost bound to confound everyone. Stony knows what he needs and wants to see, but conveying anyone elses' thoughts is a hard task for most people, and imo it's always best to completely simplify the whole process to suit yourself rather than try to replicate another persons brain. If you look at my chart for example for AN on the h4 t/f, you will see just 4 lines. The magenta and yellow lines are the cloud lines actually with a shorter setting, because ultimately Ichimoku is basically a means of predicting future movement and can therefore be considered as sophisticated shifted mas and that means they can be interpreted as such and used in the same way as ma crossovers are often used (so far as I am concerned anyway). So, working on that principle, we can get an advanced notification of a signal via the coloured lines, then we simply wait for a similar set of black and white lines to cross as the entry signal using another set of Ichimoku. The example chart shows the early warning, and soon after we get the entry signal to sell. All of this covered by just 4 lines instead of all the confusing stuff etc etc. Of course, it's a question of what the individual finds easiest, because as said, we all have different brains, thus we all see things differently.
