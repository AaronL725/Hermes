DHR - Daily Half Range Strategy Conditions: If price has moved half of the 10 day average range on a given day a trade is entered for 5 pips profit, stoploss 20-30 pips. One trade a day only . For a buy trade price must have moved half the daily average range (ADR(period 10)), measured from the LOW of the current day. For a sell trade price must have moved half the daily average range (ADR(period 10)), measured from the HIGH of the current day. No trades for the first 1-2 hours after market opens to avoid false breakouts. I trade this method live (small lot size) on EURUSD 15M chart. Rough and ready testing on EURUSD 15M Chart shows the following results for 2023 Jan to 11 May 2023 so far. Attached Image (click to enlarge) Still improvements to be made regarding the few losing trades; testing a few differet options; martingale is a potential option as appears to lose only one trade in a row and then win the next at least 3-4 trades. Potentially a filter for high impact news as whiplash can be more than half daily ATR(10). Maybe look at days of the week etc. Prefer no other indicators are to be used. Only price, and range such as highs and lows. The rest is via trade management. This is a pure rule based system. The template and indicator list and template in post 1 will be updated periodically as the method is made more precise. Why the Small Pip Target??????????? Firstly, this is a pure rule based method, so it can be scientifically and statistically measured in a r eproducible way. The goal is to have profitable trades consistently day after day. This needs to be based on probability and risk calculations. The small pip target is based on probability and risk. If a market moves on average, say 50-100 pips per day and a trader wants 100 pips the probability of achieving 100 pips is statistically very low. Due to spread, the inherent way price moves, where the trade is entered etc...... It is like trying to pin the tail on the donkey for trade entry and then saying ok price will now move 100 pips from this point in a certain direction. As the number of pips per trader decrease the probability of achieving those pips increases; inversley proportional. The obvious starting point is the minimum average range per day, as given the above, we are unlikely to get 100% of this value given trade entry is like pinning a tail on the donkey. Looking at standard distribution, mean, CV value etc.... a 5 pip target has a very high probability taking into account spread. Another way to look at the pip target is... If you knew the probability of reaching the 5 pip target was ~&gt;95%; how many lots would you trade per pip? This way of thinking allows for automation of the method with probability of success leaning towards the trader.

Q&amp;A Session: Q5) Why Only One Trade a Day? A5) The 50% ADR(period) (or any other % ADR(period) chosen), point can only be reached / passed through once in a given day; therefore a single trade. Which ever line price crosses first will be the ADR(10) 50% mark. No guessing, price shows us the direction and potential entry point. Whether to take the trade depends on your probablity and risk analysis. Attached Image (click to enlarge) Q6) Why is buy line (blue) sometimes below the open price of the day and vice versa for the sell line (red)? A6) The buy line is calculated from the low of the day plus 50% of ADR(10). If the distance between the open of the day and the low of day is greater than the 50% ADR(10), (or any other % ADR(period) chosen), the buy line will be positioned below the open of day. Vice versa for the sell line. This is why the method can not be back tested by looking at charts. Attached Image (click to enlarge)

The place a trade may occur is known. Whilst waiting for price to reach this point we need to build a story as to why the trade should be entered. The Hypothesis is I should not enter this trade. For each trade the hypothesis must be disproved with enough evidence each day, each trade. Hence the saying "Whenever there is any doubt, there is no doubt". We use our time to gather the evidence, look at market structure, recognise if the market is slow today e.g. ADR is expanding slowly, no true direction. If the market is slow why? The market maybe waiting for something to happen, look if there is news today based on the indice etc being traded. As part of gathering evidence I use the DHR ATR(10) H1, the DHR Strength Indicator and sometimes if not sure ATR(1) M15 with a level line at 0.0015. If you look at ATR(1) M15 over the past days, see that a spike of ~15 pips occurs when there is initial momentum / potential breakout. However at the time of the breakout the ATR(1) value will obviously be less in real time. The above is why my preference is to focus on one currency pair, as each day is part of a continuing story and so understand how the pair moves. I'm in the process of updating the DHR Line Indicator, with chart display of % ATR and strength with the option to select the currency pair. If you were trading manually, a breakeven could potentially have been set by moving the SL line as price did hang around the ~3-4 pip profit mark for awhile. The current move away from the line maybe a pullback within the downward trend, price will let us know. This is why I post / share my daily trade / no-trade everyday TP or SL with notes as to why a trade was entered or not. I hope it helps.

Hi 8tango, Apologises for the late response. The Percentage ADR tells us how close we are to the 50% mark at which a trade is taken if confirmed. The idea is when 50% of ADR(10) is reached a trade is taken if confirmed. The lines tells us if we are looking for a buy or sell trade at the 50% of ADR(10). The buy line is 50% of ADR from low of the day and vice versa for the sell. So it is a kind of a race to see which line is reached first. For reasons unknown to me, the 50% of ADR(10) region is where the action occurs; be it breakout or failure to breakout. I have tried all different ADR values and have always come back to ADR(10). Hope that makes sense.

The idea was to find the half way point of total price movement in a day from high to low. Therefore we use the high and low of the day. As we are unable to predict the half way point, I used the average of the daily range for the last 10 days and calcaulte what is half of ADR(10). Hence the 50% mark. Testing showed this worked best. ATR and ADR are the same just that ADR is atr set to a time frame of a day. So we are waiting for the 50% of ADR to reached therefore there is no specific time just when the line has been touched. Whichever line is touched first determine if buy or sell. Only one trade a day.
