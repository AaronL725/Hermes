The soul of this indi is the ZIG-ZAG. Without it, it's nothing. So how does this Indi plot the zig-zag. The zig-zag is based on oscilator indis or moving averages. There are several options you can choose in this indi. You can select which music score you want to use in order to plot the zig-zag 1. MACD 2. Stochastic 3. Double_MA_Xover 4. Double_EMA_Xover 5. EMA_Price_Xover So let say you select Double_MA_Xover of 5 and 10. 5 MA is the fast MA and 10 is the slow MA. Make sure you select the tempo to "Custom" as seen in the screenshot. In this example you input 5,10 into the tempo field as seen in the screenshot Bullish crossover is whenever the fast MA is higher than the slow MA. Bearish crossover is whenever the fast MA is lower than the slow MA. so when ever there's a bullish crossover, the price will look for the lowest point between the previous crossover until the recent (new/fresh) crossover. This logic is apply to the bearish crossover, but it will look for the highest point. Attached Image (click to enlarge) Correction to the screenshot -_- Sorry

--- 帖子 #4 ---
Second component of this indi is the FIBEX or MOM (Momentum). Each ZigZag point has 2 FibExes; top and bottom. In the screenshot below the FibEx is highlighted in black circle Attached Image (click to enlarge) This act as a fence of a particular move. Let say the fibex is set to 1.382. The market moved 10 pips to the upside. So the FibEx is 10*1.382 = 13.8 pips or 0.00138 pips. So what does Fibex do? Fibex is a great way to identify breakout. This is very important to master because market doesn't always trending. Most of the time it moves sideway. So therefore you need a tool that you can rely to identify a breakout. Fibex is similar to bollinger band does. It has contraction and expansion. When there's expansion that mean the momentum is big. When there's contraction that mean the momentum is decrease. When there's a contraction in FibEx, that mean there is no clear up/down trend. We could be in a reversal or a trend continuation. This is a great opportunity to take side. I recommend to use 1.382, you can decrease or increase the FibEx to your own. Make sure it is set bigger than 1. Attached Image (click to enlarge)

What is the meaning of the following??? 1. Bullish with high volume 2. Bullish with low volume 3. Bearish with high volume 4. Bearish with low volume First we have to understand how it is calculated. Volume = SUM(Accumulation/Distribution indi + On Balance Volume) of that particular frame. Let say there are 15 candles. The indis will sum all 15 candle's AD and OBV indi value. HOWEVER , if the value of A/D or OBV value is &lt;0 or in a negative value, it will be multiplied by -1 in order to get the positive value. Let say there's a candle A/D indi value is -60000. Zigcharm will multiply it by -1 = 60000. In the screenshot below you can see 2 information highlighted in black box. Those information are the sum of volume and the difference between that frame to the previous frame. Attached Image (click to enlarge) About the calculation of volume diff data window Attached Image (click to enlarge) So let's get back to the question, Bullish with high volume....? When we have that, that mean that particular bullish frame has higher volume in relation to the previous bullish move. Attached Image (click to enlarge) In another example above. Divergence and hidden divergence is calculated based on the volume of each frame. Bearish divergence appears whenever we have a higher high (bullish frame is higher than the previous bullish frame). And the latest bullish frame volume is lower (colored in pale green) than the previous bullish frame, so that mean it's a bearish divergence. Bullish divergence is when we have a lower low and the frame color is pale red. Bullish hidden divergence is when we have a bullsih move and followed with a correction. That correction colored in pale red, that mean we have a bullish trend continuation or bullish hidden divergence. Bearish hidden divergence is when we have a bearish moveand followed with a correction. That correction colored in bright green, that mean we have a bearish trend continuation or bearish hidden divergence. Attached Image

--- 帖子 #13 ---
The 7th Component is the SuperTrend. The supertrend is always on. If you want to turn it off, simply change the color #11 and 12 to None Attached Image (click to enlarge) Here is the logic/algorithm of super trend 1. Identify the last frame point 2. Retrieve it's MOM or the FibEx value 3. If it's bullish add the frame's+MOM For bearish case subtract it Bullish Dynamic SR = last frame's price - MOM Bearish Dynamic SR = last frame's price + MOM 4. Increase the Bullish dynamic SR every time the current price close is higher than the previous candle's high. Decrease the bearish dynamic SR every time the current price close is lower than the previous candle's low. Bullish Dynamic SR = (Current candle close - previous candle close)+dynamic SR Bearish Dynamic SR = (Previous candle close - current candle close)-dynamic SR 5. When a candle broke particular dynamic SR, repeat #1 What does it use for? It is use to identify the change of trend. It complements the frame of reference when it is drawn with volume on. It also can be use as S/R like in the example below. Attached Image (click to enlarge) The example above takes the end/tail part of the supertrend as the S/R, highlighted in black circle. Break below or close below the lowest/highest S/R of that, determine the trend. If it break the lowest part, that mean a bearish trend likely hood is higher therefore look for a short setup probably it is best to place trade after a correction. That is just one example on how to use SuperTrend indi.

Sideway market is often described as end of a market trend. So here's how you identify a sideway trend market Attached Image (click to enlarge) Attached Image (click to enlarge) When you see both images above. In those circles you should see 3 frames getting smaller and also notice that the FibEx Up and FibEx Down contracted. Then follow with frame "X" broke Y's FibEx to the upside. When the FibEx of the previous frame got broke, you should look for a long/short position depending on which FibEx does the current frame break. If it broke the FibEx low of the previous frame, that mean look for a short position. You could place the short position after a correction of the breakout. Attached Image (click to enlarge) From the screenshot above. It is clearly we are in a uptrend move. Notice we had a breakout to the upside already. Then follow with a correction. The correction is a sideway move (a triangle pattern). Also notice the FibExs are contracting. So expect a trend contiuation to the upside or a reversal to the downside. In summary When you notice there's a contraction in the FibEx for several frames (at least 3 frames). Then expect a breakout to either side (trend reversal or trend continuation).
