An important concept of optimizations and data quality. EAs have parameter "is_open_prices_only" (as 1st parameter). It is used for optimizations to get them fast and to see 10 years best setups very fast (minutes). EA is specially designed so that if the parameter is true then all logic happens only on new bar opening moment (according to timeframe - if M5 then every 5 minutes - everything between is ignored...) When working with real ticks (or go with live trading) that parameter must be set to false... Otherwise EA will work with 2x more drawdowns. The main difference between trading contracts for difference (CFD) and share trading is that when you trade a CFD you are speculating on a market’s price without taking ownership of the underlying asset, whereas when you trade shares you need to take ownership of the underlying stocks. For real stocks (not CFDs): Commission-based account – commissions typically range between $75 and $100 each time you buy or sell. Fee-based account – you pay an annual fee, which includes the cost of advice and trading commissions. The fee is typically 1-2% of the value of your account. For CFDs , there is yearly SWAP from 1.5 to 6% depending on the broker. And might be a small commission when open deal (Darwinex it is 0.02 usd), Admiral Markets no commission but 2.5% yearly SWAP. Darwinex minimum volume for stocks is 1 lots. Admiral minimum depending on the stock might be 0.1 lots. 0.1 lots are better for scaling - for example, if you start with 1000 usd account and 1 lots volume for a deal and grow to 1100 usd (10% growth) then you can increase your trading volume to 1.1 lots. Not possible in Darwinex... you need more money to scale 10% in Darwinex. For example, start with 10000 usd and 10 lots. Then you can increase to 11 lots on 10% growth. Going short is possible only with CFDs. Anyway, small investors can't really impact company decisions with a tiny amount of real shares, therefore, do not see advantages to have 1 share of MSFT as real stock... You have to buy a lot of MSFT real shares to cover commission, for example, 75$. If 1 share cost 240 usd and you pay an additional 75$ to buy it... well... CFD then is much cheaper to buy if small amounts. Or you need to buy at least 3750 usd volume of shares to cover 75$ expenses (2%). And then will have to pay another 75$ when you decide to sell real shares. So actually min volume is 7500 usd. But yes if you hold shares for several years then it is better to have real shares. Because with CFD you will have to pay every year SWAPs (actually a small fraction every day... but no more than 2.5% yearly if Admiral or 1.5% if Darwinex). Trade as a private client on FCA regulated broker - and in Europe your capital (85000 GBP) will be 100% protected even if the broker no more exists (not very possible for FCA regulated brokers but ok - shit happens). Darwinex however offers not only FCA-regulated guarantees but even more - up to 1 mil. GBP guaranteed with the insurance company. Darwinex insured all capital they work - so you get more guarantees.
