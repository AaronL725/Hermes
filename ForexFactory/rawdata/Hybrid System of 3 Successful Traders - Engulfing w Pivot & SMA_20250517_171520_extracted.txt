No worries I don't use many indicators and I want my charts to be as clean as possible but another indicator which would be of value to people who trade this strategy is the Average daily range. This would be very useful because I can see how much a pair has traveled during the day, in relation to its longer term average daily range and in relation to the top and bottom of the day. The H number tells us how far price has traveled from the top of the day and the L number tells us how far price has traveled from the bottom of the day. Adding the H+L numbers in our head, gives us the daily number of pips and if price is let's say 10 pips from the low and 70 pips from the high then the pair has traveled 80 pips today If the 6M number (6 monthly) = 150pips and today we have done 80 pips then the probability for price stopping and reversing is not the highest possible. Or we could use it in this way. If our pair had done 145 pips and we are a few hours from the end of the NY session then the possibility of a reversal is much better I also I'd like to see what the pair did yesterday (the Y number) because if yesterday we had a huge daily run, then it is possible that today we may have a small day and/or even a reversing day.
