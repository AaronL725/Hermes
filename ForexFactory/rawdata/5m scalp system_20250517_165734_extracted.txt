Back testing: You put the system to test by applying it on previous data. This can be done visually by scrolling through a chart or in a simulator. Using a simulator is better. When scrolling through a chart visually, you may get some info on the direction of the next bar as the current bar is always closed and the indicators being used reflect that. This may lead to the testing providing misleading results. Forward testing: Trading the system live in a demo account. Some people prefer using a cent account, where a dollar goes a long way! Here are some simulators for MT4's strategy tester: http://www.forexfactory.com/showthread.php?t=70742 http://www.forexfactory.com/showthread.php?t=60849 http://www.forexfactory.com/showthread.php?t=289899

Hi Number Seven, Opening a trade: When a BUYSTOP is submitted with a pending price of HIGH+Spread, the order will be filled when ASK hits the HIGH+Spread. Visually, on the chart, the BID may or may not have cleared the HIGH, depending on the spread at the point of fill, and it may give the impression that the trade is entered early . On the other hand, a SELLSTOP with pending price of LOW-Spread, visually, does not give that impression. In other words, because of the spread, a BUYSTOP and SELLSTOP opening order do not seem similar. To make them more "symmetric", does it make sense to open a SELLSTOP with pending price of LOW, instead of LOW-spread? Stoploss: For a short trade, the stoploss is specified to be the HIGH. The stoploss will be triggered when the ASK hits the HIGH. Visually, the BID has not hit the HIGH yet, so it may give the impression that the trade is stopped out prematurely. For a long trade, this problem does not arise. In other words, because of the spread, the stoploss execution does not seem symmetric. Does it make sense to include a spread for the short trade in the stoploss, i.e., the stoploss will be HIGH + Spread, instead of just the HIGH? Personally, I feel that how the spreads are handled may have impact on trading, and I would like to see how your interpretation differs from what has been implemented in Aurora. Thanks!
