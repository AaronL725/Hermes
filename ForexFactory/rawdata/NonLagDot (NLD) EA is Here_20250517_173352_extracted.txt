I have been using a Non-Lag Indicator EA in conjunction with renko for a bit, before this thread at least. I have been finding success with it, however it has nothing to do with fine tuning the EA necessarily but making judgments on my side. I find that semi-automated systems work the best, meaning that factors such as when to trade, what pairs to trade, what direction to trade, etc, should be made by the human, but the actual execution is better off in the hands of a robot. The biggest downside to renko is that it takes many trades during ranging periods. One modification I have taken with regards to the EA is only take trades once two dots of opposite color have closed, this limits small losses during ranging periods. Other suggestions that have worked for me: -Try using different brick sizes, perhaps 5% of the ADR I find allows you to eliminate most range, and also get in early on trends. -What has helped me the most is to not ignore fundamentals when setting up the EA. I scan all of my selected pairs and see which one is most likely to move. If it is stuck in between two areas of strong support and resistance, I won't put the EA on that pair because it is likely to range. If the pair has S/R or Pivot points that are far away from each other, I will run the EA on that pair because that means price will generally move substantially until one of those levels are reached. Just simple things to try and avoid the chop. -Being aware of the upcoming news also helps eliminate chop. Sometimes the market won't pick a side until news is released so be careful with that. Running renko 2 or 3 days when there isn't big news can make a lot of $$$. -The last indicator I use it in conjunction with is stochastic. If the one hour stoch is pointing up from oversold, or vice versa, I will select the option to make the EA take either ONLY LONGS OR ONLY SHORTS. This way it does not buy/sell on the retrace, which is where the small losses are accumulated. Renko is perfect for this indicator/EA in my opinion because it moves with price instead of 15 min / 30 min candles where the trade might be taken after the move is over.
