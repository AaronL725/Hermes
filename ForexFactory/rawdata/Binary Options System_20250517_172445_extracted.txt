There is only one good Binary Broker available to US traders, and that is NADEX. They are based in Chicago and are regulated by the government. If you are outside the US, then IG Markets is the best broker. Binary options are good ONLY if you are certain about price/direction, but not certain about the volatility and dont want to get stopped out because of whipsaws etc. They are only better than "regular" markets (such as stocks, forex etc) under this one condition. Think of them as a "tool" in your trader's toolbox that you use for very specific situations. But beyond that, lets not kid ourselves...these binary contracts are not "options" at all...real Options contracts give you the OPTION to exercise the right to purchase stocks at a certain price if they move in your favor...hence the name OPTION...these binaries do not give you any right to purchase the underlying security. Binaries themselves are nothing more than "bets" on the direction of a certain stock or asset. They are as a matter of fact, the same thing as a sports bet, like the over/unders, and were actually modeled on the sports betting industry. Besides, if one wants to trade direction on any stock, you can just open a regular options trading account at ThinkOrSwim, or TradeMonster, and you can sell Bull Put Spreads or Bear Call Spreads and get basically the same directional play, with a tighter spread than what NADEX offers. And you are not limited to the list of assets that most binary 'brokers' offer to you, and you can customize the time frame as well. They may not offer 'hourly' or 'daily' bets, most options are traded with either monthly, weekly, or yearly (known as LEAPS) expirations but you can just close the position when your price target has been reached. You can even trade options-on-forex by just trading the options for the various currency ETF's.
