--- 帖子 #40 ---
Yes, you are correct. During the countdown phase, a new Setup phase can occur. Demark call this Recycling (two or more consecutive setups of the same kind). This means: - you have to check all the time for a new setup - determine which setup to be used for countdown (the most recent setup or ignore smaller-sized recycled Setups by comparing the differences between lowest true low and highest true high of both setups and choosing the setup with the bigger difference) - Recycling will cancel one of those two Setups The "absolute prerequest" is in fact the signal to start a new Setup phase. A buy Setup is initiated where a price bar's close that is greater than the close four price bars earlier is immediately succeeded (on the next price bar) by a close that is less than the close four price bars earlier. &lt;C[4]&lt; p&gt;

--- 帖子 #68 ---
1. The setup is perfected when the market registered the minimum number of bars where, in case of a buy setup, the close is less then four bars ago. But the setup phase can continue after bar 9 until the market registers a price flipor a neutral reding. This thing is important if TDSEQ is used with other indicators. The bars need to be consecutive. 2. The countdown is perfected when there are 13 bars where the close is less then two bars ago. 13 bars is maximum and also the minimum number of bars for a countdown. The bars don't need to be consecutive. 3. For recycling, there are two options: a. the recent setup is maintined and from this setup will start count the countdown b. the other option is called "Ignore lowest true high/low''. You find the Highest true high and the lowest true low for both setups. Compute the difference for both setups, and maintain on chart, for start of countdown, the setup with a bigger size.

--- 帖子 #91 ---
You could use a Wolfe Wave (if Present) to calculate objective but how Tom DeMark uses his TD Lines to project where market may go to is: for an upside target take the amount calculated from lowest low underneath Supply Line and the same point on the line itself, and then add that amount to breakout point at the line. Naturally reverse for downside target. In other words if trendline is drawn from 100 to 50 and lowest low underneath the line is 47.50 and straight above this low the line is at 77.50 then add 30.00 to breakout point (say 53.00) crossing back above trendline. So target would be $83.00 Like any projection, it is not always hit. Please note that in his books he has 4 different trendline qualifiers. One of the best trading investments I have ever made was getting all three of his books.
