--- 帖子 #128 ---
Hi Since Yes two ways of trading, using different methods... The trading methods here allow you trade up to previous high and also beyond previous high or low in a down trend. One is simply using rejection wick high as the resistance line from previous high and when price breaks apply trades with 10 pips stops and take profit at the horizontal line and/or trade many positions and let other trades run... The other is a trade based on recent volatility using the Average true range indicator (ATR). When price is trending the horizontal line represents the most recent high in the trend, price has then pulled back and until that previous high is broken there is chance of trend change... So using the ATR at a setting of 4 it will give you the average range of pip move for the last 4 candles... You then apply that range number to your trade and apply stop at 60 EMA... This trading method has an extremely high win rate but the down side is that the majority of the time the risk is far greater than the reward.. but like I said, extremely high win rate.. Always do your own back testing though buddy.
