I use the following indicators: - Pivot points: daily, weekly and monthly. - Trend lines &amp; parallel lines. - Price action / support &amp; resistance. - Fibs. - Other traders. They may have different strategies but the same turning-point as i have. - Cross pair confluence. - Elliott wave theory. My strategy: - I need to have a long term view on a pair to know in what direction i want to trade. - I want to risk max 10 pips by trading off the 1 minute time-frame and let my trade run after i moved my stop loss to break even. - When target is far away i may add in lower time-frame if nothing else important is going on. - I enter my trade only where there is confluence. - My main trade indicators are the daily, weekly and monthly pivot point on entry but i may enter without it in my confluence. - When rejection is poor, i walk away from the trade even if i have some loss. - I don't enter trades on Friday later upon the day because then traders close their positions and price becomes less predictable and between Friday and Monday a gap can occur which can leave me with a loss larger then my stop-loss. Will look how price is rejected. Can close trade and buy trade back on Monday. - I only want to trade pairs with a low spread.
