This thread is dedicated to a a particular breakout idea I've had but never really traded. I imagine it would suit autotraders very well but unfortunately I don't have the coding skills to explore this further. I believe it has similarities to a Donchian Breakout. The basic rules are as follows: Longs: If the current bar's close is higher than the highs of the previous 2 bars, then enter at a break of this bar. Shorts: If the current bar's close is lower than the lows of the previous 2 bars, then enter at a break of this bar. If the trade isn't executed on the very next bar, then it is cancelled. Take Profit and Stop Loss: Both set at whatever value ATR(50) is at the close of the current bar. An option could be to set TP at 2xATR. The theory is that although there will be less winners, in times of big movements, the profits can stack on. In this way, BE could be occur when the trade is in profit by 1xATR. Pairs to Trade: I anticipate this works better on pairs that tend to trend more than others or break out at different market opening times. Time Frame: I like the look of the 1 hour charts but signals occur on any time frame. This is a trading idea that I'd like to experiment with and see if a profitable system could be made out of it. I welcome all constructive contributions! examples to follow...

Been looking at lots of breakout ideas of late, combining fractals, swings, various indicators etc. Stumbled across the 10 pip stretchy which basically involves identifying a 3bar swing high or low and then buying the open of the next bar. I like the idea of it and decided to see how it stacks up when combined with the 2bar close rule that is the main them of this thread. Upon first glance on a few pairs it seems to look pretty nice although I have no idea how it would hold up on a thorough backtest. The original pdf I found here: http://masrukhin.yolasite.com/resources/10%20PIPS.pdf You can see in on the attached chart the 1s represent the bars which formed a higher high or low compared with the 2 bars on either side. Unlike in the pdf, I have named the bar to the left of the swing high/low as Bar 1. For a buy, Bar 2 needs to close above the highs of both bar 1 and the Swing Low. Entry is at open of Bar 3, stop placed just under the low of Bar 2. Take profit can be personal preference. Perhaps 1:1 or 1:1.5. Reverse this for a sell. Bar 2 close should be lower than the lows of bar 1 and the swing high. Stop placed just above the high of bar 2, Entry at open of Bar 3. Please take a look. I think this has more promise than what was initially outlined earlier in this thread. Have a nice weekend!
