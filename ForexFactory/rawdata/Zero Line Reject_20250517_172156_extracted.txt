Time frame: M5 for Asia (8pm New York), London (0900 GMT) and New York 8 am eastern (the activity gets serious often around 0930). Step 1: This is what I choose to do... I'm already on the M30 chart not looking at price, but momentum. You want a strongly trending market. If within your chosen basket of instruments, you find nothing trending strongly according to the CCI/s, go get a sandwich, read a book, cut grass, do your dishes, sit on your hands, you get the point. Come back in 30 minutes and look again. If I'm trading a hidden divergence on the H1, I look at MOMENTUM on the Daily only. Indicators: CCI 14 and 50, standard default settings... ATR 15 I often use for stops (ATR2) Moving averages: 14 EMA, 34 EMA and the 204 EMA all set on the close. The 204 gives me an idea of what the 34 is doing on the M30 chart. Sell setup: Strong momentum on the M30, I wait for 1 of 3 things to happen on the M5 chart. With separation of all 3 moving averages, I wait to see price increase to in between the 34 and the 204. At this point, the 50 CCI will be above the zero line. When price returns lower back down through the 34, the 50 CCI will also cross back down below the zero line. This is called a zero line reject. The 2nd and 3rd things to happen are when price again creates longs again and returns to either the 14 or the 34. As this happens, the CCI will make it's way back up towards the zero line of the 14 CCI. The CCI does not have to cross the zero line to be a valid setup. I wont trade until I see 3 bullish bars. I risk 1% on these trades. I often take half of that risk off when I have reached 1:1. I attempt to re-enter the market. If I am able to re-enter, it will be on the M5. One hour hidden divergence: I check the H4 and the H1 for setups with my 14 CCI, I have used a stochastics 9,5,5 in the past. I use a fibonacci fan to filter these trades, nothing closing beyond the 88% level. I then plot my fib extension for a visual representation of what "could happen". I plot a measured move to help with a profit target. I go to the M15 and sometimes the m5 chart to plot my trend line. Never execute these without a trend line drawn. I take a portion of my profit at 1:1. No one knows if the trade will work out. Take profit of some kind as soon as possible. I risk 1% on these trades and often hold these overnight.
