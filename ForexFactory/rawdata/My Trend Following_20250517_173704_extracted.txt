Following the trend works really well when the market is trending the problem is most of the time a market is not trending it is in a trading range. This means that your opportunities to trade are limited and you will not be in a trade in one particular market for most of the time. Sounds straight forward but unless you can identify early on when a price is beginning to trend you can lose more than you make. This is because entering a trade when the market is not beginning to trend will result in being stopped out for a loss. First you need a criteria to identify when a price is leaving the state of being in a trading range and entering the state of being in trend. Unless you have this clear then you will lose.
