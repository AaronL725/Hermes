First, let's take a look at a daily chart for my favorite fozzy pair, the CAD/JPY. I zoomed out and removed the Vegas Daily Tunnel but left the fib levels in place. It's not difficult to see how this pair trended between the outer second and third levels on the high side and the same levels on the low side for the past seven months. Only once did the price decline to the fourth level. (This kind of price action can be seen on numerous pairs, making them the best to trade with Fozzy. But that's not to say the other pairs can't be Fozzy traded successfully as well.) Now let's add the cross of the RSI and MA. The indicator on my chart shows ALL crosses, ignoring the Bollinger Bands. I do not automatically accept the green arrow as a signal; I actually "look" at the cross as well as the corresponding values for the RSI and MA. I want a definite cross before seriously considering a trade; no weak (or almost crosses) for me! OK.............let's go back and look at the green arrows and the corresponding Daily candle that generated the "complete" cross. Notice how often the Daily candle that generated the cross and paved the way for a successful Fozzy trade is a large candle? After these large "signal" candles, there may only be one or two smaller candles before the strength of the trade has been exhausted. Not always, but often times this is what happens. Wouldn't it be nice (Beach Boys anyone?) if we could enter the trade confidently before the Daily candle and the Fozzy cross it generates are complete? If that day is a large price move, we should be able to capture "some" of that move by entering a trade during the day. In effect we would be "getting a jump" on the Fozzy Daily Trade. I believe that can be done safely and successfully. The next post will take us one step closer to that reality.
