I will show you here two basic rules how to identify the most important zones: 1. The more the price moves away from a zone the higher is the probability that when it comes back it will react to this zone 2. The less time price spends at a level the higher is the probability that when it comes back it will react to this zone. Attached Image (click to enlarge) 1. You can see here a big move away on the H1 EURCHF. After that the base of this move became a strong supply level. You can see that the price reacts several times to this level. 2. The price spent just a little time on this level because the sellers came in quickly. You can see afterwards that it bounces back two times and the price doesn't break through! With those two hints it will be a little easier to identify "good" and "bad" zones. Keep on posting and asking, I will try to answer every question!
