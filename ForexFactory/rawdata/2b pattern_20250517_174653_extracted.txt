--- 帖子 #6 ---
now the first part is the entry. first of all we must look for a previous swing point. we must look at the relative size of the bars contained in the pattern and we look at a meaningfull bullish or bearish bar(depending on the double top double bottom situation) which stops in it's evolution around the level of the double bottom/top . in the case of double top we look for a meaningfull bullish bar and if the price comes back and we also have the divergence present we put the sell entry at the bullish bar's low. in the double bottom situation we look for a bearish bar and we place buy orders if the prices comes back to the high of the bearich bar. now remember that we are looking for a rapid reaction to the double top/bottom area. ideally it should be a pin bar. but if we have the bullish or the bearish bar we start counting to 4. this is the magic number. if we do not have an order triggered in 4 bars including the initial bar then we cancel the order.

80% of 2b's reach 0.61 fib expansion. at least 60% of them reach 100 fib expansion.so you take half of the profit at 0.61 and you put your stop at break eveb. now you have 0.3 of the initial stop as a profit. if it goes beyond that and you reach 100 then you exit fully and then you have another 0.5. total is 0.8. it is very important that after the first breakout bar you move your stop at 0.2-0.3 percent of the initial full stop. let's see examples.
