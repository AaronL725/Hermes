Engulfer Trading System (ETS) uses engulfing as an entry method where trader can select the number (n) of engulfed candles (e.g. 1, 2, 3 ... etc). The system generates both trend reversal signals and trend continuation signals after pull backs. It uses high RR (Reward to Risk) ratio, 3 or higher. The engulfing method implemented here is as follows: - The system acts on the CURRENT candle to benefit from the break out momentum - Bullish Engulfer (LONG Signal --&gt; Buy): * The current candle price should exceed the Highs of all (n) previous candles * The immediate previous candle should be bearish * The Low of the current candle should be less or equal than the close of all (n) previous candles - Bearish Engulfer (SHORT Signal --&gt; Sell): * The current candle price should exceed the Lows of all (n) previous candles * The immediate previous candle should be bullish * The High of the current candle should be greater or equal than the close of all (n) previous candles // Entry &amp; Exit :-------------------------------------------- - Buy on Bullish Engulfer with stop loss at previous candle low , profit target = RR x Stop Loss - Sell on Bearish Engulfer, with stop loss at previous candle high , profit target = RR x Stop Loss // Position Sizing :----------------------------------------- - Position size can be fixed or automatically calculated by the EA based on Stop Loss and Risk percentage of account free margin, for each trade. // Trade Timing :--------------------------------------------- - EA has a trading time filter (start hour to end hour Inclusive) - Time filter is used only for opening trades. While closing is based on SL or TP which can happen any time. - Time used is Broker Time - I found better results during European Session GMT 6 to GMT 16, so for example my broker time is GMT + 3, my timing filter will be 9 to 19 ... and so on. // Constraints :--------------------------------------------- - Only one position at a time per pair/tf - Only one trade can be taken on the same candle Sample Chart : EUR/USD H1 with (n) candles set to 3 and RR set to 2 Attached Image (click to enlarge) Tester report : EUR/USD H1 - 2015 with (n) candles set to 2 and RR set to 5 Attached Image (click to enlarge) Enhancement Requests ; kohones : an option to close all positions at profit target "x" (Money / Equity Percentage) abokwaik : Min SL, Max SL, TS, Min Engulfer Range MathTrader7 : Break Even option. , No Trades on same bar where last trade was closed. wattaman : entry on retrace %

Its should be logical and it should be affordable. In general sense, a logical SL would be a one which is few Pips(25% of your SL) behind recent low for long positions and above recent high for Short positions. Similarly affordable SLs are those which do not make your TP too high. Which if hit can cause serious damage to your gains and in case if your following an RR system, high SL will make your TP difficult to hit. If you are getting into situations where your SL is becoming high, then make the entry pending until it enters a zone where SL is affordable. For me, I will not enter on first rejection. because 95% of time the area from where price is rejected will be re-tested. I will wait till the price hit my area of liking where i can achieve lowest SL preferably for 1:3 or 1:4 RR. Some traders believe in a fix number of Pips for SL. but to me this is not the right approach. 10 PIPs for AUD pairs means too much and 10 PIPS for GBP pairs means nothing. Therefore, i almost always found it better to calculate the SL based on patterns i am entering. (c) Suitable Entry Point Entering straight into a position based on engulfing on previous bars will not bring too much fruit. I try to explain this with an example.
