I have been using price action as exit... On a 4 hour chart if price bridges the tunnel then I will exit my trades. This is somewhat conservative as there are times the price may recover and continue, but for the majority of the time this is an excellent exit sign that occurs before the actuall MA cross. I also pay very close attention to convergence/divergence on stochastics (adjust your stoch. to best fit your currency pair). http://i18.tinypic.com/4fw5v2p.jpg You can also look for RSI failure swings or even Stoch failure swings.

First all, I found your EA used future data as indicator: double RSI=iRSI(NULL,0,21,PRICE_CLOSE, 0 ); double Price = iOpen(Symbol(), NULL, 0 ); double Trigger=iMA(NULL,0,6,0,MODE_EMA,PRICE_CLOSE, 0 ); double MA1=iMA(NULL,0,18,0,MODE_EMA,PRICE_CLOSE, 0 ); double MA2=iMA(NULL,0,28,0,MODE_EMA,PRICE_CLOSE, 0 ); Reference to bar[0] is not reliable since it will create beautyful backtest result but poor live trading. All bar[0] refered EA are looking good (mostly 'too good') with backtesting but all of them had failed in forward testing. If you change the bar[0] reference to bar[1], the result will be more trustable: double RSI=iRSI(NULL,0,21,PRICE_CLOSE, 1 ); double Price = iClose (Symbol(), NULL, 1 ); double Trigger=iMA(NULL,0,6,0,MODE_EMA,PRICE_CLOSE, 1 ); double MA1=iMA(NULL,0,18,0,MODE_EMA,PRICE_CLOSE, 1 ); double MA2=iMA(NULL,0,28,0,MODE_EMA,PRICE_CLOSE, 1 ); By the way, although change it to bar[1] will produce many loss and lower the win rate, your EA sill show very good final profit. I think it is very worth to continue your development and optimization.

--- 帖子 #168 ---
I'd also like to suggest that if you have an indicator which repaints, the problem may be that the main loop runs in the wrong direction. If you see that the code shows it going from 0 to a certain limit with i++, then it is starting in the present and calculating back in time, which is wrong because you end up "knowing" the future in the past. To fix this, make the loop go from the limit down to 0 or 1 (depending on the algorithm) with i--, and you'll have to change the inner loop to make up for the correct time flow (past to future, with the present knowing the past). That suggestion is obviously for coders or people who may ask coders to fix repainting indicators if their loop is wrong. Noam
