There is no such thing like strong, very strong etc. support or resistance (S/R). There is only potential. High and low swing points are in most cases potential S/R. Just make sure your lines are objective and not based on where it “seems” better. Start from swing high/low. We don’t know if price will respect those levels or not until it have been confirmed by a close nearby or broken by 0.5 % (half of what we use on TL). In the presented chart you will see some examples of how S/R drawing can look. I don’t care to put any line up before I see a price-level have been tested and respected at least 2 times. That’s it.

--- 帖子 #3 ---
I use close-price chart to make it easier. To apply any form of trend-lines (from now on TL), you need to have a trend. So what is a trend? I’ll show you. When price forms higher highs (HH) and higher lows (HL) you are in an uptrend. If price forms lower lows (LL) and lower highs (LH) we are in a downtrend. But this doesn’t help much? It does if you attach TL to some of these points. But never look at the right side of your chart when drawing TL. Don’t draw from the lowest low to the highest high. Why? As Marber says, that’s where you’re hoping it will be. And hope doesn’t help you much. 3 criteria’s to help you drawn TL correct; 1. Find the lowest point on your chart (the lowest point from where price started to make new highs) 2. A high followed by HL which is higher than the bottom and a 3. Higher high, higher than the first one. Reverse this process in a downtrend. Trend reversal; An uptrend reverses/end when the TL has been broken (by close-price) by 1% (in many occasions, you’ll find that the 1% rule act as support/resistance against price) AND the last significant LOW on the way up have been broken (at three successive closes, if we should follow the book) and visa versa for downtrend. Notice that a sign of a broken trend is not necessary a trade-signal.

--- 帖子 #6 ---
Double tops/bottoms; This pattern occurs after a (significant) bull/bear run. In my experience, I find double tops/bottoms (DT/DB) to perform best when the distance between are lesser than 1 month. I always pay attention to how both indicators are channelling. It increased the success-rate when both indicators are (already) making new highs and higher lows or lower lows/lower highs (divergence) when DT/DB forms. Pattern aborts when price have closed above/below a confirmed DT/DB. I play this a bit more defensive. So I will close half my position if price closes below/above the highest/lowest (peak/rally) confirmation-point on DT/DB. The (high) difference between tops/bottoms shouldn't exceed 100 pips on most pairs. I posted this eur/nzd trade here

--- 帖子 #7 ---
Pattern that signs exhaustion of the currently bull/bear run and a break to the opposite direction of the wedge is expected. But there is no such thing as always in this market. Calculation of potential targets; Downward wedge; Take the lower TL and copy its angle and attach it to the starting-point of upper TL. Upward wedge; Take the higher TL and copy its angle. Then attach it to the starting-point of lower TL. To copy any line in MT4, mark line and press down CTRL bottom while you drag it.

--- 帖子 #10 ---
Known as continuation-pattern. To have a flag, we need a flag-pole. And 2 touches on both lines, at least. The flag-pole cannot have (in a down-market) more than 2 successive up-days and visa versa for up-market. Sides of the flag can be parallel, converge or diverge so pennants are included in the term flag. Flag aborts if; In a downward market, we see 3 successive up-days, in a upward market, we see 3 successive down-days 1 % close above the pattern Fails to reach its potential target in the same daily range as the flag-pole had. Target I use is half the distance of the flag-pole. Exit: If price enters back into the pattern, I’ll close half the position and rest if price closes inside.
