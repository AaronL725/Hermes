--- 帖子 #14 ---
Technically, they don’t repaint. However, when you do multi-time frame analysis the indicators will ‘recalculate’. In this case the Frama ribbon and the SuperTrend candles are weekly indicators, and Chris is trading on the daily timeframe. So the indicators are ‘calculating’ each day, but their values only become final at the end of each week. So for example, a dot may appear on the Frama ribbon any day during the week and not be there at the end of the week. Nothing wrong with it, because that is how the indicators work in MTF analysis. However, doing a visual back test on MTF analysis always looks better than what it really is when trading forward on a day by day basis.

Here is a tutorial (for those who need it) on how I am figuring out the xSuperTrend settings for various TFs. First- figure out the two TFs you wish to use. Second- open two charts with the lower TF. Third- put the Frama Ribbon on both charts with the settings from post 1 and change the TF (on the frama ribbon settings) to the higher TF you are using. Fourth- put xSupertrend on both charts with the settings from post 1, on one of the charts set to your desired higher TF and on the other chart set it to the current TF. Fifth- on the chart with the xsupertrend set to current TF change the settings until you get it to match the higher TF chart. Hope that is helpful. Chris

OK let's try this If we want to trade the daily chart the frama ribbon will need to take its signals from the weekly TF. The xsupertrend also wants to take its signals from the weekly TF, but doing this we get the colour change signals too late (having to wait for the weekly candle to close) So...we need to open 2 daily charts both as described above. Then on one chart you will change the TF of the xsupetrend to current (which will be the daily TF) On that same chart you will change the "supertrend period" and the "supertrend multiplier" until it looks close enough to the unaltered chart. When you are happy with how it looks close the other daily chart and the chart that is left (the adjusted one) is now ready to trade with. This is how you would set up charts for whatever TF you want to trade with. Chris
