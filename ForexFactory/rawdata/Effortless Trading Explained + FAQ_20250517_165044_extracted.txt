In order to draw a trend line we need two points, each of which has two co-ordinates - time and price. We know both of these coordinates for the first point so we just need to define a time and a price for the second point. By defining the second point in terms of time and price, it will always hold the same point on the chart irrespective of the zoom or stretch of the screen. Time is easy one, we just project a fixed amount of time into the future - eg 24hours. Price is harder. The method I've come up with is to use a proportion of the ATR for each day of time we want to project forward. So the price element of the second co-ordinate is calculated as: Second TrendLine point = First point price +- {Number of days projected forward * PriceATRFactor * ATR} (+ or -) depends on if it is a Long or Short line. So essentially for each day across you go, you step in price by a factor of the ATR. Using ATR rather than absolutes allows this method to be applicable across different pairs with the same settings.
