Welcome to my thread where I present a system I have been testing for the past month. This is a very simple daily system I have been working with to catch minor and major trending moves or waves in price action. Over the past 12 months I have been reading every forex trading book I can get my hands on and testing all sorts of ideas, both back testing and forward testing, along with money management and psychology. I feel this little system really captures the most fundamental aspects of the movement of price action in forex – the investment of the market and the probability of what will happen next. With a couple of rules I get myself into a place where the probability weighs in my favour. And really I feel that is what it’s all about. Price can only really go in 1 of 2 directions. So you really have 50% chance of getting it right. So then it’s all about finding an extra few % and how you limit those that go against you and exploit those that you win. During my time of study I have found that the market is not so trending as it once was. There appear to be more toing and froing and so this system is designed to find a change in trend early and get in and out in time for to make a profit before it turns again. About pips. Pips mean nothing to me. I do not go for 20 pips a day or 100 pips per week or whatever. If I did then some trades where I need stop of 150pips or such will scare me. Instead I always set a risk limit per trade. I use 2% of my bank. So I take 2% of my account and divide into the size of my stop to find the position size. So the most I can lose if things go bad is 2%. I do this on any system and any trade so it doesn’t matter if the stop is 23 pips or 223 pips. The intent is to have something mechanical that takes a lot of the analysis and decision making out of entering and exiting trades. I focus on the main pairs such as GBPUSD, GBPAUD, EURUSD, EURAUD, AUDUSD but any pair could be used. My aim in sharing this is not only to share something I think can work for other people but to also discuss and find methods for avoiding bad signals and riding out good ones.

So, this system is simple: 1. Open a Daily chart – this is simply an attempt to create something that only requires a few minutes a day to review and action. 2. Place 20 EMA – some sort of line in the sand. 20 days represents to me around a month of prior movement and therefore time for a change to occur. Short term trends changing regularly. It also means that on a longer term trend we may be able to make pips off a correction or retrace before again making pips off the continuation. 3. LONG SIGNAL occurs when a complete candle CLOSES above the 20EMA (i.e. no part of the candle touches the 20EMA). This tells us there is investment from the market to move price up. We do not know whether or not this could be a long term thing or a simple spike. Now you are looking for short candles (close is lower than open) in order to identify possible confirmation. https://lh4.googleusercontent.com/-z...00/simple1.jpg 4. ENTRY. 2 Options here. 1st option is to place a BUY ORDER either half way up the short candle from the close to the high, or at the high of that candle. The reason we do this is to avoid any bad reversals or longer term corrections. If price continues short, we can cancel the entry order and do the same once the next candle completes (as long as it is not a new SIGNAL SHORT). If it moves in our direction, we give up a few pips to ensure we are with the market. Don’t worry. If we are with the market, they will drive us where we want to be. The more aggressive entry to immediately after that candle closes. https://lh3.googleusercontent.com/-4...40/simple3.jpg 2nd option is to wait for another short candle and use that for the entry in the same way. This 2nd correction is more confirmation but often these short term trends we want to spot are so violent, there is no 2nd short candle as you can see from the examples. Another more aggressive entry is to enter on the high of the signal candle BEFORE the reversal candle whether or not the signal candle is long or short, place and entry order for the following day at the high of that signal candle. https://lh6.googleusercontent.com/-F...40/simple9.jpg 5. MANAGE THE TRADE. If you have a set target then leave the position as we will either be right or wrong and you should trust yourself and the system to reveal that for you. This position is to get a quick win within a few days and locks in pips. You can adjust the stop as discussed below daily if you wish but leave the target as is. If you have no target then manage it daily by adjusting the stop and letting it run for as long as it can. This is to ensure we ride any longer term trends for maximum pips, locking in profit on a regular basis and exiting when things go flat or turn.

IMPORTANT NOTES: STOPS. A couple of ideas with the stops is to set it at 100pips or the last swing low or use a 2 candle low explained. I tend to use the 2 candle low or 100pips, whichever is smaller. Now you know the stop, you can set your position size. https://lh3.googleusercontent.com/-D...40/simple4.jpg TARGETS. Again there are a couple of options here. Either uses the same value as the stop or leave it open and review the trade daily as explained. TRAILING STOPS. Do not adjust the stop until the trade is at break even or in profit. I lean towards a stop based on 1.5 to 2 or even 3 previous candles called a Count Back Line. So for a LONG position the stop is at the bottom of the previous 2 candles where the #2 candle must be lower than the #1 previous. This may be 3 or 4 or even 5 candles further back. Draw a horizontal line at the low of the previous candle and look back and see if further back another candle is lower, then use that 2nd low as your stop. As the low of any new candle moves higher, this stop is moved up. This allows any sideways movement or small retrace to be tolerated before any continuation of trend returns. Another idea is to always sit 50 or 75 or 100 pips behind. So if the close of today is at +125 you place your stop at +75 or +50 or +25 and do that daily. You may have some of your own ideas on stops here so try them out. https://lh5.googleusercontent.com/-8...40/simple5.jpg https://lh3.googleusercontent.com/-w...40/simple6.jpg EXITS. Always let the trade exit when it wants to either using your set target or by hitting the original or trailing stop. No decision necessary. Always stay out of the market when price touches penetrates the 20EMA. This is too ranging and means the market does not really know what it wants to do other than sit back and see what everyone else wants to do. We do the same. RENTRY. Further trades can be taken in the same direction at close of any red candles as explained above – HOWEVER I have noticed that if the correcting short candle EXCEEDS the length of the previous candle such as above, it is a sign the market may be rethinking its progress and may be looking to consolidate at that price or reverse. Keep an eye on the candles. They will most likely move to and fro from long to short to long to short like above and then either continue trend or not. Look for 2 or 3 consecutive candles to show things may not be so clear to trade in. EXPECIALLY if price moves back to touch the 20EMA. The rule I follow is to re-enter on close of any other short candles as long as they are not longer than it’s previous long candle or touching the 20EMA. Consecutive short candles means multiple positions entered but I cap it at 3 max per pair.

This triggers and activates with a stop in place not at yesterdays low but at the previous lower low (remember to count 2 lower lows backwards) at the low of 21/8 for -104 pips. 29/8 this open positions moves a little up immediately but only a short while before dropping again. 30/8 the open position has its stop moved up to the low of 28/8 at -55 pips. Today’s candle was also a reversal candle that was bigger than it’s predecessor thereby signalled an immediate exit. Remember we can always enter again off this candle tomorrow. POSITION CLOSED -30 pips. 31/8 we see a standing BUY triggered with it’s stop at the low of 28/8 for -70 pips. This position blasts through and upwards. At the close of this candle the stop is moved to the low of 30/8 for -61 pips. 3/9 candle slows but still increases enabling a higher low so the trailing stop moves to the low of 31/8 for -50 pips. 4/9 is a new reverse candle but not too big so another entry tomorrow may be achieved. The low of this candle is higher again so the stop of the open positions moves again. This time to the low of 3/9 but because that is so close to the 4/9 I choose to leave it at 31/8 or -50 pips. 5/9 drops a little but then increases for the day triggering our 2nd position. Both positions now increase steadily taking their respective stops with them and locking in guaranteed profit after a few days. 10/9 is a short candle and allows for a possible entry tomorrow. It does not short enough to take our 2 stops out which are now aligned at the same point – the low of 7/9 for +95 and +38 respectively. And the candle does not signal a stalling trend so we are happy. 11/9 a 3rd position is activated with a stop at the low of 7/9 for -80 pips. 14/9 end of the day shows all 3 trades still in with all 3 trades using the same low of 13/9 as their trailing stop, locking in a guaranteed +240, +180 and +85 pips in the bank. Now remember all trades are a maximum risk of 2% of the bank: 20/8 trade with a stop of -65 pips (2%) returned +150 pips or +7.40% 28/8 trade with a stop of -104 pips (2%) returned -30 pips or -0.56% 31/8 trade with a stop of -70 pips (2%) still open with a guaranteed +240 pips or +6.84% 5/9 trade with a stop of -104 pips (2%) still open with a guaranteed +180 pips or +3.46% 11/9 trade with a stop of -70 pips (2%) still open with a guaranteed +85 pips or +2.42% So in the past 1 month on 1 pair I have risked 10% of my account on 5 trades returning an increase in my account of 19.56% so far.

RANGING OPTION If a candle finishes the day after crossing both the 10EMA and the 20EMA in that day, it can be probable that the recent trend is over and either a new trend will develop (ie a new full candle on the other side of the 10EMA) or it will spend a little time going to and fro along the 10EMA and 20EMA lines. Let me give an example: Assume an asset with the price at midnight at $10. It rises to $11 at 12:00, then falls to $9 at 18:00 pm, rises again to $10 at 24:00 pm, and rises further to $12 at 12:00 the next day. If your price data begins exactly at midnight, this day candle is a Doji. If it begins at 12:00 however, it's a hammer with open = high = $11, low = $9 and close = %12. If it begins at 8:00, it's a normal looking candle with a $1 body. A Doji or Hammer of a 24-hour traded asset does not suggest a faltering trend, as it's a random product of the start hour of your price data series. The idea that a Doji indicates a faultering trend is from stock trading. Stock prices normally only change during trading hours between 9:00 and 16:30 pm, so a Doji of a stock price does not depend on the start hour as long as the candle covers the whole trading period.

Thanks for the prompter. Here are the cleaned up more cautious system rules: 1. Open a Daily chart – this is simply an attempt to create something that only requires a few minutes a day to review and action. 2. Place a 10EMA and 21EMA – some sort of line in the sand. 21 days represents to me around a month of prior movement and therefore time for a change to occur. Short term trends changing regularly. It also means that on a longer term trend we may be able to make pips off a correction or retrace before again making pips off the continuation. 3. LONG SIGNAL occurs when a complete candle OPENS and CLOSES above the 10EMA and the 21EMA (i.e. no part of the candle touches the EMAs) AND the 10EMA is above and moving away from the 21EMA. This tells us there is investment from the market to move price up. We do not know whether or not this could be a long term thing or a simple spike. Now you are looking for short candles (close is lower than open) in order to identify possible confirmation. 4. ENTRY. 2 Options here. 1st option is to place a BUY ORDER at the high of that signal candle. The reason we do this is to avoid any bad reversals or longer term corrections. If price goes short, we can cancel the entry order and do the same once the next candle completes (as long as it is not a new SIGNAL SHORT ie below both EMAs). If it moves in our direction, we give up a few pips to ensure we are with the market. Don’t worry. If we are with the market, they will drive us where we want to be. 2nd option is to wait for a short candle and use that for the entry in the same way. Place the order half way along the length of this candle or at the high. Often these short term trends we want to spot are so violent, there is no short candle for a while so using a combination of both options is best. 5. MANAGE THE TRADE. If you have a set target then leave the position as we will either be right or wrong and you should trust yourself and the system to reveal that for you. This position is to get a quick win within a few days and locks in pips. You can adjust the stop as discussed below daily if you wish but leave the target as is. If you have no target then manage it daily by adjusting the stop and letting it run for as long as it can. This is to ensure we ride any longer term trends for maximum pips, locking in profit on a regular basis and exiting when things go flat or turn. 6. ADD TO THE TRADE. Add another position once the current position is up the value risked or when you see new reversal candle signals in step 4. Or simply add another position at every +100p or when stop gets to break even or when stop goes up by 100p IMPORTANT NOTES: STOPS. A couple of ideas with the stops is to set it at 100pips or the last swing low or use a 2 candle low explained. I tend to use the 2 candle low or 100pips, whichever is smaller. Now you know the stop, you can set your position size. Divide your risk by the stop size and then by 10 to get the position to take. ie 2% of account = $100 divided by 100pip stop = $1 divided by 10 = 0.10. So take a lot size of 0.1 or 10,000 units. TARGETS. Again there are a couple of options here. Either uses the same value as the stop or leave it open and review the trade daily as explained. TRAILING STOPS. Do not adjust the stop until the trade is at break even or in profit. I lean towards a stop based on 1.5 to 2 or even 3 previous candles called a Count Back Line. So for a LONG position the stop is at the bottom of the previous 2 candles where the #2 candle must be lower than the #1 previous. This may be 3 or 4 or even 5 candles further back. Draw a horizontal line at the low of the previous candle and look back and see if further back another candle is lower, then use that 2nd low as your stop. As the low of any new candle moves higher, this stop is moved up. This allows any sideways movement or small retrace to be tolerated before any continuation of trend returns. Another idea is to always sit 50 or 75 or 100 pips behind. So if the close of today is at +125 you place your stop at +75 or +50 or +25 and do that daily. You may have some of your own ideas on stops here so try them out. EXITS. Always let the trade exit when it wants to either using your set target or by hitting the original or trailing stop. No decision necessary. Always stay out of the market when price touches penetrates the 21EMA. This is too ranging and means the market does not really know what it wants to do other than sit back and see what everyone else wants to do. We do the same. Be careful or lines of support and resistance (I look at Weekly and Daily charts for these), as well as pychological/round numbers and the candle formations themselves such as pins and dojis. If 2 retrace candles in a row, use 2 candle high for re-entry in case a new trend is developing. 2 retrace candles in a row suggests good support for reversal of the trend. https://lh4.googleusercontent.com/-P...ethewavev2.jpg https://picasaweb.google.com/lh/phot...eat=directlink

Been away for a while looking at signals and confirmations and measuring success from variations of those and I have found something that I want to forward test for a while. CHARTS 1. Use Daily chart 2. Add 6MA and 21MA. These are used for signals and identifying the current trend no matter how big or small. A large retracement to me is a small trend. 3. Add ATR(%). This is the average candle size over the past week. I use this for trailing stops. RULES 4. SIGNAL occurs when a full or almost a full candle closes above for LONG (or below for SHORT) the 6MA. 1 x UP is awarded. (Short signal would be 1 x DOWN awarded.) 5. CONFIRMATION for this signal occurs when you check the angle of the 6MA on the signal candle AND the 2 previous candles. 1 x UP for each up angle and 1 x DOWN each down angle. You may have to check the actual value of the 6MA as there is no flats. 6. FINAL CONFIRMATION occurs when all this happens ABOVE for LONG (or BELOW for SHORT) the 21MA thereby being in an exisiting uptrend. 1 x UP if in uptrend or 1 x DOWN if in down trend. So add up the confirmations, ie UP (singal above 6MA), UP (angle of 6MA on signal candle), UP (angle of 6MA on previous candle), UP (angle of 6MA on 2nd previous candle), UP (6MA is currently above 21MA) = 5 x UP, 0 x DOWN = PERFECT LONG SIGNAL so enter ASAP. ENTRY If the count is 5 to 0 then this is perfect signal so enter ASAP If the count is 4 to 1 then this is ok so enter ASAP If the count is 3 to 2 then wait for next candle to confirm either direction and recount If the count is 2 to 3 then wait for next candle to confirm either direction and recount If the count is 1 to 4 then wait for next candle to confirm either direction and recount MANAGEMENT Use ATR value on signal candle rounded up to nearst 10p as a trailing stop. Use this with your 2% of account to find your position size. ADD additional position (same size) as an entry order at 50p or 75p or even the current ATR value ahead so when your trade is up by that amount, another position is added with same trailing stop rules. Keep adding positions along the move until it stops out. LIVE EXAMPLE Here's an example I am currently in now. I flagged the candle on 20/11 as possible move to the upside. Score was 3/2.
