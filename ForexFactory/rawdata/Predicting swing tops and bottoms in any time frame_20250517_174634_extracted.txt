What I do when trading is I analyze both 30 min and 15 min time frames and pick the turning point in 15 min time frame. Then I watch closely when it comes closer to the turning point. If the last few 1 hour bars have made lower lows or lower highs then I figure out the turning point is going to be a bottom. If the last few 1 hour bars have made higher highs or higher lows then I figure out the turning point is going to be a top. So when the 15 min bars show a price reversal, I take the trade to that direction and keep my stoploss below the last low or above the last high. Usually the stoploss maximum stoploss is around 20 pips. This worked very well for me but I was busy with my full time job and couldnt do much trading later.
