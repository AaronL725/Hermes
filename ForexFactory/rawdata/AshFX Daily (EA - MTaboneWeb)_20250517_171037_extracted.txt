As a trend gets closer to the end the PSAR gets closer and closer to the low/high of the current bar. Of course there is always the chance that your trade will go bad right away. Also the way I would implement the PSAR trailing stop would be by moving the stop loss up to the level of the previous bar's PSAR value so it wouldn't be happening during the current bar. The PSAR just seems to ride nicely above or below the current direction and from what I've read is what it was initially developed for just that.
