THE SYSTEM The idea for this system 1st came to me when I was experimenting with a variant of 10p3. With 10p3 I saw lots of little profits and then eventually the system did as martingale does and bombed out with a huge loss. I thought to myself, why not reverse this, take lots of little losses, and rather a huge loss when the system goes wrong, a huge profit when it goes right. The entry is as the 10p3 I first used, from there on in all the logic is reversed. Here are the input variables at default values and their meanings explained: ALL profit and loss prices are in the format of 0.00150, this way it works on 4 and 5 digit brokers, also my programming skills are not up to scratch. TakeProfit = 0.01500; This value is the ultimate goal, ALL your placed orders will have this value. StopLoss = 0.00100; From the 2nd order onwards, all orders will be modified to have a SL of this many pips. InitialStopLoss = 0.00100; The first order only can have a different value from the rest, when the 2nd order is placed the stoploss will be as the "StopLoss" value. Multiplier = 2; The next order will be this many times bigger than the last, set to 1 if you dont want order sizes to increase. If can be 1.1 or 2.34, whatever you want, but not zero. LotSize = 0.01; The size of the first order. MagicNumber = 801369; I'm sure you all know about these by now. Pips = 0.00300; When your 1st order is in profit by this much, your 2nd order will open and 3rd 4th etc until your TakeProfit value hits(or is so close the order is not valid) ReverseSignal = false; This reverses the entry trade from a buy to a sell and vice versa. slippage = 0; Slippage, remember in 0.0001 format.
