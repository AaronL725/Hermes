The raw FX spreads you see on our platform are the exact best bid and the best ask being quoted from among those competing providers without any markups* http://i.imgur.com/gk8W0tr.jpg * In some instances, accounts for clients of certain intermediaries are subject to a markup. The reason liquidity providers will widen their spreads during some news events is to manage the increased risk to them of quoting prices at such times.
