Let me add to this (and my remarks are also somewhat related to Masmp_FX's post #49: I don't like to scale out either, but what does make sense in my opinion is to scale in, i.e. add to positions when a first position develops favorably. What I do is this: if a position develops according to my game plan, at some point in time I will be able to trail my SL to break-even. From this moment on, the existing position poses no longer any threat to my account. From that moment on, if the indicators signal a trend continuation, I add to my position. Two requirements here: break-even for my first position needs to be ascertained and the new position (size / SL-distance) needs to remain within my set risk profile limit, meaning that the new position should not pose a greater risk than the initial position was. This second position gets a trailing SL, just like the first one. If the trend continues, I continue to add to my position in this way, but never before the existing position(s) have become risk-free. Now on to post #49: Masmp_FX, there no reason to not add to a position that you kept one or more days, as long as a trend continuation is expected and your existing position(s) pose no longer a risk to your account. Does this method sound logical to you guys &amp; gals? For me it's the best way I could come up with to deploy my resources effectively. take care Ohka
