ENTRIES : EU-M1 TF These are the same for buys and sells ... 1. ZERO LAG MACD will usually cross first, this is the early warning. 2. VQ will usually change color next, this is our get ready. 3. Price crosses and closes on the opposite side of the 20EMA...this is GO...... A . The offending candle then becomes our starting point..no entry is made yet. B. We use a limit order with a buffer to enter. This way the market brings us in on its own momentum, and we avoid some of the whipsaws that are associated with moving average cross over systems. 1)Bufferfor buy : 2 pips plus spread above the offending candle 2)Sl for buy : 1 pip below the offending candle . 3)Buffer for sell: 2 pips plus spread below the offending candle 4)Sl for sell: one pip above the offending candle OK..entries and stop losses are covered...now for.... MONEY MANAGEMENT: The old money management thing.....this is where it can go completely wrong for you, no matter what system you use. This makes managing my trades very simple and takes the most stress out of my day that i can. Now on to what I do to manage my money (trades).... 1. I strictly adhere to my stop losses ....they usually arent that large to begin with so even if they are hit Im not hurt. Never move your Sl when the trade is going against you, never. 2. I take some profit off of the table when the trade goes my way , this means for me closing half of my trade after a 1:1 risk to reward ratio is hit, moving the remaining unit's stop to BE and enjoying my free trade. See? Friggin Simple, right? 3. I never risk more than one percent of my account on any trade ...ever. 4 . Hello Lesso. Thank you for a great scalping solution. In the hope of giving back, you will find an indicator attached that does a logical "and" of your signals to produce an integrated signal. There are also a couple of extensions that you will find detailed below. The external parameters are as follows: 1. EarlySignalOnly -- setting this to "true" simply causes an alert to be given before the close of the "offending" candle. It simply gives one a little more time to prepare, but it also gives more false signals. 2. FreshSignalOnly -- setting this to true gives a signal only when the 'offending' candle opens and closes on either side of the EMA20 and the other selected filter conditions are all satisfied. 3. EMA20_Filter_Enable -- setting this to 'true' gives a 'long' signal when price is ablove the EMA20 and a 'short' signal when price is below the EMA20. 4. MACD_Filter_Enable -- setting this to 'true' signals 'long' when MACD &gt; MACD_Signal and 'short' when MACD &lt; MACD_Signal. 5. VQ_Signal_Enable -- setting this to true enables the VQ functionallity. I have worked with VQ a lot lately, and have experimented with multiple VQ lengths. The implementation provide here provides for the specification of two independent VQ lenghts. You can select one or two VQ lengths. If you select two lengths (like 5 and 8, for example), a 'long' signal is given when both VQ5 and VQ8 signal long. If you select only one, the other must be '0'. On 1m charts, I have come to use only one VQ (I like VQ5 on 1m). On 5m and above charts, it can be useful to use two VQ values. You must use the version of VQ Indicator (VQmod) attached as it supports my iCustom calls to MTF VQ. 6. SMA200_Trend_Filter -- setting this to true gives a 'long' signal when price is above the SMA200 and the slope of the SMA200 is &gt; min200Slope. A 'short' signal is given when price is below the SMA200 and the slope of the SMA200 is less than -min200Slope. min200Slope is express in pips. I would suggest that you either leave the value of min200Slope at '0.0' (no slope filtering) or experiment with values around 0.05 pips on a 1m chart. I am currently not using this filter because it tends to make one either miss trades or get in trades late. I simple eyeball the SMA200 line before deciding to take a trade. 7. Up to two independent time windows are provided for. If you use a time window, you can only specify the starting hour and the ending hour as integers. No minutes are provided for. 8. Pop-up alerts and email alerts are provided for. Load both of the attached indicators into the experts.indicators directory. To use trade time windows, you set "TradeWindowEnable" to true. Two separate trade windows are provided and can be enabled/disabled separately. Window1Enable and Window2Enable. Trade times are specified in even hours and are "Server Time". They reference the time as kept by your brokers server. So if you want to trade only during the London session, and your brokers London session starts at 3am and ends at 10am (like Oanda ), you would set TradeWindowEnable = true, Window1Enable = true, TradeStartTime1 = 3, and TradeStopTime1 = 10. I personally enable a trade time before I go to bed at night for the start of the London Open (3am my time). I enable email alerts and my cell phone wakes me up when the first signal hits after 3am. With regard to your use of v1.1, the TradeWindows do not work properly in that version. They were fixed in v1.3. Also, v1.1 was missing some signals when a bar came close to the 20ema but did not cross it, and then opened on the next candle on the other side of the 20ema. This should have been a setup if the other indicators also lined up, but it was missed in v1.1. Versions 1.2 and 1.3 corrected this by giving a signal if a candle closes within a sepcified distance from the ema20. The distance is specified in the external parameter "TouchDistance". It is set to a default of 0.1 pip, but I have been using 1.0 pip today. When I am trading 1m charts, I am sitting right right at my computer.
