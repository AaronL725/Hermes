--- 帖子 #15 ---
Lets say the 4hr is currently showing a down trend.... As soon as a peak of that down trend is broken, I consider the uptrend to begin. Some may call this risky by going to early, however, this gets us in right at the beginning of the trend and opens up the ability to start finding entries into the new trend very early on the 5min. In the case of a 4hr trend change, the 30min will always agree with it at the beginning. The 30min is used to determine if it is a good time to trade that trend or not. Here is an example: 4hr is trending up. 30min slows and goes into a range, so we do not trade the 5min until the 30min has resumed trending. 30min resumes its trend by breaking out of the range, and we start taking entries on the 5min again. Think of the 30min as our on/off switch to taking 5min entries. If 30min is showing undecided, ranging, or trending against the 4hr, our 5min trading is OFF. If 30min is trending well and in agreement with the 4hr, our 5min trading is ON. So to answer your question, there really is no set number of waves I use to determine if the pair or time frame is trending, as soon as I see a break of a wave, I assume the pair has made it's decision.
