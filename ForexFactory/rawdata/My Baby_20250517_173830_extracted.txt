If you need a background education on this then find "Billy Ray Valentines" "No Brainer trades" free threads. They should be still right here on Forex Factory. So number 1, what is it? Its horizontal lines,,, Its fuel its support its resistance, its strong, its obvious... What is it? Its OBVIOUS. Its those levels you are too scared to take even though you just know they will work but the market seems too strong, too bullish to go against it. Well that's fine for us All we need is some fuel We are not risking an artery, we are risking a scratch. When we try and catch this falling knife or rising knife we are simply opening a weak position with full intentions of losing. We don't care. We just know that this level has some energy. Time frame? Look for Monthly, Weekly, Daily and if you're desperate H4 levels No lower. FUEL Say it FUEL a small reaction on a Weekly chart is maybe 100 200 pips regardless of trend,, FUEL When the market hits our level we buy/sell with a SL under the next round number. 2 - The Turn So we've found our level and opened our first position. We have zero expectations at this point. The win rate will be less than 50% and maybe closer to 30 or 40% after 100 trades but as we are eventually waiting for 5-20:1 R:R we don't mind. But we also want to act consistently from very solid levels and not throw money away needlessly. So learn to find quality levels that you think could bounce at least 100 pips ( 300-1000 ideally) "The turn" is the first sign that our level is good and we will get a confirming reaction from around our price point. It will be a very sharp reversal that counters the trend. Again there's no easy way to explain this other than it will be obvious. I guess the best way to explain would be a strong intraday trend against the current daily trend (for example). It could happen in one 4 hour bar or maybe a daily bar. You're just looking for that sharp Turn There's no need to overthink it is simply our price level reacting as we hoped and confirming us to move to stage 3 In the chart below you can see the Turn quite clearly I have marked a blue down arrow above the Turn Bar. The daily bar turns sharply from our level with the momentum fierce against the up trend. The turn confirms our top and we now bring our SL down to the top at 114.45 and SELL more. Money Management Through our entire trade we stick to our nominated risk level. In the example I have chosen .5% risk which is $25 (on a 5k account). Remember we are going to lose trades it needs to be an amount you would throw away for fun relative to your account size. (Not that we will be doing that!). But this is important for the mind set. THE FUEL We sold at 114.42 with our Stop Loss behind the next round number at 115.05 Our first position was opened with a 63 pip Stop Loss when we divide our Risk of $25 by our 63 pip Stop loss we get a position size of .39. $25/ 63 pips = .39. So our first position was opened at 40 cents a pip. THE TURN On the turn the market potentially topped at 114.45 so we Sell again at 113.59 (this is the point you decide that the turn is there, it could have been lower or higher in reality) and bring our STOP down to the top at 114.45. So... Keeping our risk at $25. We bring our Stop loss down to around our entry point (-3 pips) so our risk before we have more risk to spend as 3 pips multiplied by 40 cents is $1.20 we have $23.80 to spend on our second position. Our second position will share the same stop loss as our first position at 114.45. We are selling 113.59 and so our Stop loss on our second position is 86 pips. We have $23.80 to spend and therefore our second position size will be 30 cents per pip ($23.80 / 86 = .27). We now have 2 positions open fora total of 70 cents per pip. Our risk is $25 and at the time of opening our second position we are in 83 pips profit and $33 profit.

3 - Momentum Reversal So after we have the turn we want to see that momentum hold, and as the up trend tries to continue we want to see it do so weaker than the Turn.. Again, this should be obvious.. And usually best seen on lower time frames.. We draw a fib from the top of our trade and wait for a touch of the retracement levels to then make an assessment on the momentum. In the Chart below you see the Turn is 3 bars down and the attempt to resume the trend moves up slowly over 5 bars, the pin bar at the bottom may have made us hesitant but subsequent bars push momentum into the bearish favor .. Once we are satisfied that the Turn has held it's momentum i.e Momentum Reversal, we bring our stops down to the closest resistance that is behind the 61.8 fib level (114.05) and SELL more at the 38.2% fib level (113.80) . We keep our $25 risk and sell the 3rd position accordingly just as explained in the Money Management post . The Fibonacci is just to keep things mechanical and non discretionary. We will always add to the trade at the 31.8% level and trail our stops behind the 61.8% (find a resistance level that is closest to it. The fib will be anchored on the top through the entire trade in this case at 114.36 In this case the stops come down to 114.05 and from this point our we are in our trade and progress to the Mechanical Pyramid.. Attached Image (click to enlarge) Money Management Market Price on our 3rd position (Momentum Reversal) is 113.80 with Stops at 114.05 so 25 pips risk. We need to work out how much to sell so... Position 1 FUEL - .40 per pip at 114.42 Stops are at 114.05 our risk is +63 pips (.40 x 63 = +$25.2) Position 2 TURN - .30 per pip at 113.59 Stops are at 114.05 our risk is -46 pips (.3 x 40 = $-12) Remember we keep a constant risk of $25 so for position 3 we have $25 + position 1 ($25.2) - Position 2 ($12) = $38.2 Position 3 MR - Our stop is 25 pips and we have $38.2 to spend so ($38 / 25 pips = $1.50 per pip) So we now have 3 positions open for a total size of $2.20 per pip our total risk is $25

4 - Mechanical Pyramid So now all the hard work is done we simply manage our trade, we have eased our self into a pretty good position with $2.20 per pip at the start of a new trend with just $25 risk... we still have no expectations, we remain unpahased to what the market does. It will do this in 1 million different ways and so that is why we use our Fib tool to avoid discretionary actions. Remember if we make random decisions we must expect random results. So from this point on we simply trail our stops every time the market makes a NEW LOW, we adjust all stops at the same time trailing them behind the 61.8 fib level and always adding a new position at the 38.2. I traded this whilst working so used limit orders and price alerts. Often I would take 5 minutes at work to adjust the stops on my smart phone. But you could code something to do this. I also used a trade copier software program which was awesome to adjust everything with ease. But doing it manually is easy enough. I would check the market in the morning before work for maybe half an hour and then again when I got home. when you're in a trade you may be a little more hands on but it's quite easy to manage... In the chart below we messed around for a little while before making a new low in our trade I market it with a red X ... We leave the 100% fib level where it is and drag the 0% fib level to the new low. We can now move our stops down 10 pips. We can do some quick math in our head knowing that our pip value is currently$2.20 and we have moved all stops down from 114.05 to behind the new 61.8 fib level of 113.92 (red dashed line). That's 11 pips so at $2.20 we have gained ground or (11 x $2.20) so we have $26.2 to spend on our 4th position which we are not opening yet... We see the market retrace again to our new fib zone so with our stops already in place our limit order can open our 4th position at 113.61. With our stop at 113.92. That is a 31 pip stop loss with $26.2 to spend so our size will be (26.2/ 31 =.85) So we enter at .80 a pip.... Life is sweet we have risked only $25 but now have a total position size of $3 a pip at the beginning of a potential new trend. We still have zero expectations.. We simply repeat this process dragging the fib down any time the market makes a new low in our trade, adjusting stops and setting new limits at the 38.2% fib level...
