--- 帖子 #43 ---
So i use the following regarding timeframes: HTF - LTF MN - W1 W1 - D1 D1 - H4 H4 - M30 H1 - M15 M30 - M5 M15 - M1 (although here be a bit less strict on M1 if it comes to MAs crossing downwards before buying, since it is a 15 times smaller TF than the higher M15 TF) So you look on the HTF for all three MA's in the right order: 20EMA &gt;50EMA &gt;100 EMA, best is all pointed up (blue MA). Also price should not touch the 20EMA (strong trend). Best is when Pips29 indicator is fully blue, and all 3 CCI's are green and/or blue. Then go to LTF, in M30 HTF case, it is M5 as the LTF. There you look for: Price pullback touching the 20EMA, while all MA's are in the right order (20EMA&gt;50EMA&gt;100 EMA) Best is with a red candle (which means candle closed below 5SMA HighLow band). Then a grey candle signifies the entry candle (price closing inside 5SMA HighLow band again). Only enter if on the entry candle not all three CCI's are green: if so, this means price is maxed out, so no entry; wait for a new opportunity. In other words; minimum one of the CCI's should be another color than green to enter on the entry candle. Management: -Mark the moment all CCI's max out at the SAME moment (all CCI's are green on one single bar). On the entry timeframe (LTF) -Then you exit from this point on in parts of 6. This will keep you in trades longer, and let you add to winners: 1/6th: When Lower CCI turns blue 1/6th: When Lower CCI turns orange 1/6th: When Middle CCI turns blue 1/6th: When Middle CCI turns orange 1/6th: When Upper CCI turns blue 1/6th: When Lower CCI turns orange (the order does not matter, whichever occurs of these 6 options means close 1/6th, it sometimes happens that you will close 2-3 parts on the same candle due to multiple CCI's changing colors on the same bar, which is fine).

And the normal saying is, that we have to get the best out of the market we have. Hence people make strategies that can fit any kind of market or they trade only in certain circumstances. My take is: Make the market for tomorrows trading . So how do we make the market? Step 1: We search all instruments we possible can. Forex, Metals, Comodities, Indicies etc. Identify nicely trending instruments on HTF. I use Daily and H4. Step 2: Make a list of those best trending instruments. See? In stead of always trade the Cable or any other pair - you trade the most trending instrument that exist right now. The list is normally long and you need to narrow it to maybe a couple of instruments. Step 3: Analyse the instruments on your list. Is there any good setups that surely will give a nice day tomorrow? It is not enough that the instrument has been trending nicely for a week. You must have a reason to believe, that the instrument will continue to trend next day. Maybe even boost. Describe the setup. Write it down. This way you make yourself think a bit more and furthermore you have the analysis to check at the end of the next day if you were right in the prediction. Now you can learn and become better. Finally you have identified a trending market for the next day!!! I use a lot of tools to find the trending instruments with the setups that promise a nice day tomorrow. Some people are afraid of many indicators. Well - if all indicators agree, then you have a setup. If one indicator disagree, then find another instrument. You can filter some of the bad days this way. Step 4: The trading. Go to LTF. I use mostly M15. Use indicators to hand the trades to you. I like CCI, MACD and SuperTrend. But hey, you are in a trending market - nearly any indicators will work here.
