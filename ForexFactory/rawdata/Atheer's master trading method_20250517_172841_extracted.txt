Ather, we will still not always get the same signals because we have different Broker feeds and because the signal generating indicator has to calculate the SweetSpotGold lines. They cannot be recovered from the indicator buffers because SweetSpotGold does not populate the buffers. Additionally, SweetSpotGold does not calculate the lines to be on even increments of 10, 20, 30, 40, etc. You Get numbers like 12, 22, 32, 42, etc. SweetSpotGold.v1.1 that I posted to this thread accepts line spacing in pips (not points) and calculate the line spacing on tens boundary (10, 20, 30, etc.), the same as the signal generator calculates. We will get much closer signals, but they will not identical unless you use SweetSpotGold.v1.1 and we use the same Broker. Let me know if you still think the signals are incorrect. Your strategy produces a lot of counter trades. Why don't you use your obviously great visual abilities (this is a complement) to come up with a strategy that takes signals off the BB center line in the direction of the center line slope -- hence in the direction of the trend. Price action in a trend frequently retrace to the BB center line before continuing. Then you would, in my opinion, have a very rewarding trend following system. Good trading.
