--- 帖子 #224 ---
MT4 [Tools.Options.Email] In my screenshot (post 577) I show [ From: vForceAlerts, name@company.com ] This means when you receive the email Alert it comes from your address but with the sender's name 'VForceAlerts'. Where you see name@company.com you need to replace with your own ISP email address. This is so your ISP's mail server will accept the message as being from you. Otherwise they will block it. The To: address is more flexible, for example you can put in your mobile phone email address or whatever you like. Everywhere you see company.com you need to replace with your own ISP's domain name. For more info on email setup click the [Help] button on that MT4 window. Enjoy! RD
