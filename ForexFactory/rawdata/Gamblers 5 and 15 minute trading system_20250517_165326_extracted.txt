--- 帖子 #243 ---
Many traders avoid NFP Friday (the first Friday of every month) all day, this seems to be one of the most volatile days. Trading is very quiet before the NFP data is released at 1.30pm UK time, and then extremely volatile afterwards. As with many news releases, the volatility can be traded by placing both a long and short order 10-20 pips above and below the price immediately before the news release. I don't tend to do this as price often retreats fairly quickly after a news release back to its starting point, although the weakness in the USD yesterday continued for the rest of the session. It is also a risky strategy as a price spike may trigger the long and then within seconds the price might fall and trigger the short, effectively giving you a loss within seconds. In my (admittedly limited) forex experience, NFP Friday is best avoided completely; although I had a short position open in GBPUSD from earlier in the week, I tightened my trailing stop, and sure enough it was taken out within a minute of the news release. In hindsight I could have taken more profit by closing before the news release, but of course the news may have been positive for the USD and the price could just as easily fallen another 100 pips. The only other news which seems to have a violent impact are interest rate decisions and when Bernanke drops hints in his speeches. Either avoid trading around the red news items in the FF calendar, or at least tighten trailing stops if you already have positions open. Andy
