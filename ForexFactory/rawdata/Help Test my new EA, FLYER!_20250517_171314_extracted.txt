Ok, I am getting quite a few responses about the 25% modelling quality of my tests which make people doubt the veracity of the EA which I can understand. However, further research shows that on the M1 chart which I am running the strategy tester it is impossible to get better than 25% according to the method strategy tester uses to calculate that percentage. Quoted from the Metatrader MQ4 website (see link at: http://articles.mql4.com/93 ) Quoted from above link: "Reasoning from the same formula, the one-minute data modelled cannot be of quality exceeding 25 per cent since data of a smaller period are not used for modelling of these data. For client terminal, there are simply no data of a smaller period, but there are tick data. However, tick data are not stored in the client terminal by standard means. In themselves, one-minute data are the most detailed data about the price movement. And, using them, one can model the price movement inside of bars of larger periods. At that, discrepancy between the real price movement and the ticks modelled will not be large due to presence of one-minute reference points: Open, High, Low, Close. These reference points form the first approximation of the price movement function (the first-order approximation). The higher is the value of the period modelled, the more there are reference points and the more exact is the modelling. And vice-versa, the lower the value of the modelled period is, the lower is the modelling quality. For example, up to 240 one-minute reference points can be used to model a one-hour bar. When modelling a 5-minute bar, the amount of reference points drops to 20. As we mentioned before, there are just 4 reference points for a one-minute bar to be formed! This is why the rating of modelling quality for one-minute bars does not exceed 25 per cent. However, if one uses signals received from data of a larger timeframe when testing on one-minute timeframe, the data modelled should, of course, be rated much higher because the price movement within the bar of timeframe larger than the on-minute one is considered." End of Quote So, if you read this and understand it, it is now shown that better than 25% on the M1 is impossible. Thus, the EA test results are probably about as accurate as they can get. I hope this helps for people to understand that my results are not bogus. GirlFlyer &lt;!-- / message --&gt;
