The reason this system works best on euro is that euro move very gently, the retracement aren't too wild. On other pairs, the retracement influence the calculation of the MA's big time, so, they are not moving in nice slope. A simple moving average is just an average calculation, that's why when the price goes away from the MA, it will go back to it sooner or later. A man taught me one time; see it as a hole, each time price goes away from the MA, it is a hole to be close. It closes by the price returning to the MA, or by the MA to reach the price, or both do half way. I believe that the man who build this, his idea was to find 2 MA,s that during 1 normal day, the price will start the day close to them, then will move away from them and come back to them at the next flat period, upfully in time for the next trading session(Asia or London). At the time I wrote this message, price was exactly back at 133 MA, the hole is close. For now, we might see another move, or it will be flat until next trading session. The difficulty of this, was to find which MAs would be working. Obviously, a term too long will catch longer trend but won't give crosses daily. Too short would make more signals, but smaller movement size.
