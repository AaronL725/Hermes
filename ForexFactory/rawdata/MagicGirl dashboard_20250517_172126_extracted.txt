The thread EUR/GBP Trendmatic started by Paulus is where you'd need to check to get the details on the indicators used in the dashboard. But, basically, when the Vorticity indicator color, which is shown as "Vorti" on the dashboard matches with the TM HG0001 colors, that signifies a strong trend. However, if you just use the colors, you may be getting in at the middle or even the end of a trend. For example, right now, the CHF/JPY is all red. Hopwever, the first match of red on the Trend Magic and Vorticity indicator was at 13:20, which was 5 hours ago when the pair was at 89.33, and it's now at 88.49, or a 87 pip gain. My suggestion is to use the dashboard as a screen for several pairs you want to consider trading. It lets you know which pairs are trending strongly. Of course, everyone wants to get in at the start, and this is why you need to have your charts set up separately with the indicators discussed on the EUR/GBP thread. You really only need two indicators, and these are red light, blue light. When they match, you trade. Simple as coloring.
