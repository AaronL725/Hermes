This system is very basic and serves as a beginning point for a lot of EA's that you will come across in this forum. Just beware of the false signals that you will surely encounter using CCI 0 as a entry for buy and sell. Many EA's have filtered out the false signals by entering a buy at around CCI 100 with the RSI above 55 and entering a sell with the CCI at -100 and RSI below 45. But CCI 14 is a very slow moving signal when most use a combination of CCI 170 and 34 or CCI 89 and 21 for a more accurate picture of the market.
