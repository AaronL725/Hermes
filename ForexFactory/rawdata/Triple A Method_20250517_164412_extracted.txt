Hi Thank you for posting your system, looks good. Just a quick observation with MTF indicators. Usually if you are observing a MTF ind that is of longer time frame than the chart bars, (ie. 1 Hr MTF Indicator on a 5 min chart) and you are watching it live, the indicator will dynamically follow the longer time frame as it changes. But if you wait for the longer time frame to finish (ie, wait until the 1 hr has finished) and either re-set indicator or re-start Meta Trader , the indicator for the entire last hr will display the last indication of the longer frame for that Hr. ( ie. it redraws the MTF indicator for the last hr (in this case) ). This makes it hart to manually back test. yellow

Dosra Signal:- If the color of the both oscillator bars changes to inverse the Pehla Signal without another new pehla signal and then again change in favor of Pehla signal we re-enter. There are two conditions in which we should trade in inverse direction of original signal 1.Stochastic Reverse Trade:- If daily stochastic are moving against the original signal and are just leaving overbought-sold area (both the stoch lines should have visited overbought oversold area). You can enter the trade in original direction if stochs again cross in our original signal direction the next day. 2.Vegas Reverse Trade:- If entry price is outside from Blue or Red Vegas line or close enough to it that risk to reward ratio is unacceptable i.e. less than fifty pips target. If signal’s entry price is more than 150 pips away from Vegas line than normal conditions may apply.
