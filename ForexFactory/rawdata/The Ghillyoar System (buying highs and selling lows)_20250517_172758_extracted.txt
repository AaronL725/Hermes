--- 帖子 #7 ---
How to work out the initial value “Buy Bench”. The initial value of the “Buy Bench” is: ( the closing price of candle 2 ) plus 30 pips How to work out the initial value “Sell Bench”. The initial value of the “Sell Bench” is: ( the closing price of candle 2 ) minus 30 pips

--- 帖子 #8 ---
The value of the Buy Bench changes. If the price of candle number 2 is higher than the current value of the “Buy Bench” then a new Buy Bench is created. The new “Buy Bench” will be equal to the value of candle 2 plus 30 pips. if ( iClose(NULL,0,2)&gt;Buy_Bench { Buy_Bench=iClose(NULL,0,2) +30 * myPoint; } The value of the Sell Bench changes. If the price of candle number 2 is lower than the current value of the “Sell Bench” then a new “Sell Bench is created. The new “Sell Bench” will be equal to the value of candle 2 minus 30 pips. if ( iClose(NULL,0,2)&lt;Sell_Bench ) { Sell_Bench=iClose(NULL,0,2) - 30 * myPoint ; }

--- 帖子 #9 ---
The Ghillyoar System: Trades are filtered: Buys are only allowed if the previous candle closed higher than the 23ema. iClose(NULL,0,1)&gt; iMA(NULL,1440,23,MaShift,MODE_EMA,MaPrice,0) Sells are only allowed if the previous candle closed lower than the 23 ema iClose(NULL,0,1)&lt; iMA(NULL,1440,23,MaShift,MODE_EMA,MaPrice,0)

Exit Rule Number One When you start trading you need to write down your opening equity e,g. 1000$ You need to use a profit target . For my back test I used a profit target of 50$ ( far from optimal ). Rule number one will only exit if the Account Equity hits 1050$. As soon as the 1050$ equity level is hit the new Equity Target is set and it is 1050$ plus 50$ = 1100$

--- 帖子 #11 ---
What Does Exit Rule Number One make of losing tades? Exit Rule Number One ignores losing trades. If The Ghillyoar System has closed a series of losing trades and our equity is now 900$ rule number one is not going to close trades when the open equity is plus 50$. Rule Number One is going to wait until the Account Equity hits the original Account Equity Target of 1050$ Very Important If Rule Number One closes all of the open trades and the system is flat then the current “Buy Bench” and the “current Sell Bench” are abandoned and are re-calculated. If Ghillyoar closes all of it’s open trades using methods 2 or 3 or 4 …………..100 then it does not re-calculate the “Buy Bench” and “The Sell Bench”.

--- 帖子 #12 ---
Exit Rule Number Two One of the two following conditions has to be satisfied. The total Open profit on buys has to be higher than 20$. or The total Open profit on sells has to be higher than 20$ In addition the following condition must be satisfied: The number of open buys must be equal to the number of open sells. Example1. We have four open trades ……….. 1 Buy and 3 sells. We are losing but Ghillyoar does nothing, After a while we open 2 more buys. We now have 3 open Buys and 3 three open sells. Either the buys or the sells are likely to be in profit by 20$ We are losing. Ghillyoar has had enough……….. we are getting no where. Ghillyoar closes all of the open trades. Important We are flat but the system does not recalculate the “Buy Bench” or the “Sell Bench” if ( ( TotalOpenProfitLong&gt;20 || TotalOpenProfitShort &gt; 20) &amp;&amp; TradesCount(OP_BUY) -TradesCount(OP_SELL) ==0 ) { myOrderClose(OP_BUY, 100, ""); myOrderClose(OP_SELL, 100, "");}

Hi Cryptofunk.............thanks for your interest in the system. I think that the published version of Ghillyoar probably works o.k. Please remember that Ghillyoar is working on two profit targets............... and they are different. 1. If the previous basket of trades was closed with a "basket profit" of 50$ then the target for the next basket ( i.e.the current basket) is a profit is 50$. When the current basket of trades hits a profit of 50$, then all open trades are closed. 2. If the last bunch of trades closed at a loss...............say 200$............then Ghillyoar will be aiming for a basket of profits that covers the previous 200$ loss....plus 50$. This device means that we are very happy to close a bunch of losing trades because there is a very high degree of probability that Ghillyoar will eventually clear the losses ............and then add on a small profit. I have tried countless variations to the published Ghillyoar...........some of them produce results that are not world shattering...............however over the long run, every single one made a profit. Of course these back tests were based on freely available data which may not be entirely trustworthy.
