Note well new traders that the “1-2-3” sequence is the move the price action makes prior to the reversal or continuation of a trend being HH, HL, LH &amp; LL. The markets price action moves in the trend or against it and either finds a strong resistance at round numbers,major levels, previous highs. This will be where price has found a strong support or resistance, this usually occurs at point "1" of the 123 pattern. Then moves in the opposite direction as a retracement to point 2, then moves back in the initial trend direction to a point 3 which represents a Fibonacci retracement level of a line being 1 to 2. It is now left up to the markets price action moves after 3 to cross level 2 and not level 1 which cancels the signal. This now will be considered the reversal or continuation mode from point 3, taking out a key level of support or resistance formed by point 2 in its direction like a raging bull.
