Hey community, The explanations for each individual part of the 7 components may seem very confusing at first. I will now go into the basic functions of each individual component and explain it later in a summary so that it should be easy to understand for most people. 1. Extreme An Extreme is a signal and not yet a trading setup. It is the first stage in the BBMA Cycle. There are 4 different types of Extreme: 1. Extreme Type A: LWMA 5 high closes above the upper BB or LWMA 5 low closes below the lower BB Attached Image (click to enlarge) 2. Extreme Type B: Bullish candle rejects upper BB or bearish candle rejects lower BB Attached Image (click to enlarge) 3. Magic Extreme: LWMA 5 + LWMA 10 closes both either upper BB or lower BB (very rare but powerfull) Attached Image (click to enlarge) 4. Advance Extreme: Price rejects EMA 50 Attached Image (click to enlarge)

3. MHV/MLV After 1. Extreme and 2. TPW have been explained, we move on to the 3rd component MHV . To keep it simple, we use MLV for the abbreviation MHV, which stands for Market Loss Volume . After several extremes have developed, the market eventually starts to lose volume. This becomes noticeable when the price no longer makes it to the upper or lower BB. This is a sign of weakness and it is then assumed that the market is losing volume. MLV can appear in many different forms and is not always easy to interpret. For example, MLV can appear as a double top, head and shoulders or a quasimodo formation. There are also other MLV appearances and I believe that this is the most difficult part of the BBMA strategy to master. There are traders who already trade the MLV directly, there are special options, but I am not sure if this is part of the original BBMA strategy. Attached Image (click to enlarge)

4. Csak/CSD After it is clear that MLV has taken place, we find our confirmation in a Csak or in english CSD (Candle Stick Direction). Since the market no longer has enough volume to continue its trend, it is now very likely that a major correction or even a complete trend change will occur. CSD occurs in two forms, a riskier variant early CSD and a normal variant CSD. After a CSD, the better setups of the BBMA strategy come into play. I will show an example with early CSD and an example with a normal CSD, whereby the early CSD is much riskier than the normal CSD. Attached Image (click to enlarge) After a CSD or an early CSD signal, we wait until the price corrects to the 510 sell zone to take a sell trade to the lower BB. Without the multi timeframe variant, the trades are not quite as good. Once I have explained all 7 stages, it is definitely best to discuss the multi timeframe variant for trades with low risk and high reward. Attached Image (click to enlarge)

Good morning dear community, I wish you all a good trading day... Multi Timeframe Analysis For the multi timeframe analysis of the BBMA Strategy, 3 time frames are initially used. You can also decide which trading style is best for you. - Position trading (MN, W1, D1) - Swing trading (D1, H4, H1) - Day trading (H4, H1, M15) - Scalping (H1, M15, M5) For the multi timeframe analysis, a checklist with various codes is used to find trading opportunities. In addition, these trading opportunities must be confirmed by this code on smaller time frames. the list for the codes looks like this: Attached Image (click to enlarge) R = Reentry E = Extreme M = MLV EE = Double Extreme Example for Swing Trading with REM code: Attached Image (click to enlarge) On D1 chart we find a Reentry after CSD sell signal. Price rises beyond the 510 sell zone and also touches the EMA 50. (R) On H4 chart we find an Extreme. (E) On H1 chart we find MLV. (M) We wait for the trading opportunity until the downward trend on the H1 chart is continued by a CSD. So we have a CSD sell signal on H1 and we wait for the correction in the 510 sell area where we then enter the sell trade. The SL could be above the MLV on H1 (50 pips) and the TP could be set on the lower BB on D1 (200 pips). To get a better risk reward ratio, you could for example take the entry on M15 or M5 instead of H1. However, there is a higher risk of being stopped out.
