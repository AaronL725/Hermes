We all agree about the great quality of the BAT ATR entry and some might feel like me, that it would be nice to increase the potential of this method, by choosing a different strategy to enter / maintain / exit the trades. To give you a maximum freedom (especially independent of you programming skills) I thought up the folloing system. The EA does offer up to 9 different trades, where the entires / stops / exits can be modified quite freely. To explain how this works, let me go back to the base of the actual system. Once one of the BAT ATR Line is broken, we find the next recent or significant low/high and draw a Fib Retracement with 0 % at the low/high and 100% at the break of the line. The trades of the official method are the following: Inserted Code [font=Courier New]Entry Exit Stop[/font] [font=Courier New]1 100 161.8 0[/font] [font=Courier New]2 61.8 100 0[/font] [font=Courier New]3 32.8 100 0[/font] Imagine now we could trade up to 9 different trades with varialble Entries and Exits Inserted Code [font=Courier New]Entry Exit Stop[/font] [font=Courier New]1 100 123.6 -100[/font] [font=Courier New]2 100 132.8 0[/font] [font=Courier New]3 100 161.8 23.6[/font] [font=Courier New]4 100 200 0 [/font] [font=Courier New]5 123.6 161.8 0[/font] [font=Courier New]6 61.8 123.6 0[/font] [font=Courier New]7 61.8 132.8 0[/font] ... etc. I think you get the point. Thats what Version 2.X will offer. You can put Entries, Stops and Exits in Percentage (even negative if you want). The system will take itself if it needs to mak a Market Order, a Stoporder or a Limitorder. Special Rules: Trade 1 must enter as Marketorder at the time of the breach of the BAT ATR line. Since you don't want to leave the pending orders open until eternity, they can be made depended on the Trade 1. The parameter Trade1WillClose influences the behaviour. It has 3 values: 0 - Do Nothing 1 - Close all pending orders (Default) 2 - Close all orders If you put the Exit to 0, the Trade will only be closed by the (Trailing) Stoploss. There have been concerns with missed entries of v1. I take them serious, and thus - unfortunatly - cannot publish the v2 before the basic problems in v1 are fixed. Have some patience and bear with me. At the same time thanks for pointing these problems out to me. I try to go through that as systematic as possible. The parameters: 5 and 4 as forseen in the original method. The entry method: The formula for a long is: "Ask&gt;=Upper &amp;&amp; High[1]&lt;=Upper" (where Upper is the actual position of the BAT ATR line) The formula for a short is: "Bid&lt;=Lower &amp;&amp; Low[1]&gt;=Lower" (where Lower is the actual position of the BAT ATR line) To explain the meaning of the formula on the example of a long entry. The EA calculates first the position of the BAT ATR line and checks if the actual Ask is greater then or equal to the BAT ATR line and at the same time the High of the previous candle is smaller then or equal to the BAT ATR line. In my humble opinion I should get all prices touching or passing the line and it should be therefore conform with the method. But please feel free to comment. So why don't we get (seemingly) all trades? Possibility 1: The systems tries to trade, but it cannot (wrong trade parameters etc.) =&gt; This means: If you see that a trade was not taken please check the expert and the journal tab and post eventual error messages. Possibility 2: My function which replaces the actual external Indicator makes mistakes. I backtested the two functions tickwise against each other and compared (automatically) the results; all was ok. It would astonish me, if it would calculate wrong, but it is still possible. To give you independence of that issue, you will have in future versions of the EA a parameter ( Use_BATATR_Indicator ) which allows you to switch between my function and the original Indicator.
