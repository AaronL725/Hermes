So if I get this correctly, in the event that you loose you double up, and if you loose again you double up again but only to 4 lots. It is a shortened martingale if I am on your wavelength and by doing so you just make winning a harder target. By that I mean you may loose 3 or 4 trades in a row which cumulatively you would have lost 1 lot, then 2 then 4 and then back to 1 which equals 8 lots, then you win and you get 1 lot back. In the end you may have more pips but your stacking of lots on losses can render your account negative, a valid argument to the pip wars. Eg, with the above scenario would require you to then win 9 trades at 1 lot to come out in front. The 9 trades would equal 450 pips while the 4 losses would equal 200. On paper the 200 pips looks great however you are marginally in front over the course of 13 trades at an ave of just 15 pips per trade with very little account growth. That may be acceptable to some but the system is at best 50/50 based on your alternate trade setup. If I am completely off the mark here then feel free to correct me, I am just basing this post on what I have read. In all honestly you would be better to build up on the winners and in the event that you get 4 trades in your favour you stack up on the other side, your reward is greater than your risk and it would stand up on a 50/50 system. Razor

Hi Traders, have been following the thread and found some interesting ideas. So I'd like to contibute some of mine. First of all: the "march" through the charts could use some improvement. The change between up-trend (+) and down-trend (-) seems random to me, without any logic. Apparently it works fine, but maybe it would work better if the market would tell in which direction to trade. My suggestion is to look at the last 3 signs and decide to trade in the direction of their majority. Example: Market +/-/+ Majority (2 out of 3) is + ---&gt; next trade + (up) -/+/- Majority is - ---&gt; next trade - (down) -/-/+ Majority is - ---&gt; next trade - You get the picture. You can stretch that to all prime numbers, but I wouldn't go further than 5 or 7 direction symbols (= + or -) at maximum. The longer the look back period, the longer this "march" will need to change the direction of your trades. Play with it a little. Next idea is inspired by "Colombino" in Post #99 ( http://tinyurl.com/y8ndleq ), especially the link to this one: http://www.win-maxx.com/basics/basics_dalem_str.html Play with it, you'll see how smooth this progression grows. You also can play with it: You don't need to use 5 consecutive losses to go to the next higher layer. I'm testing with 3 right now, and it looks promising. This progression also allows to experiment with the layers. Why not using 5 losses in a row in the first layer, but only 4 in the next, and 3 in the following? Three is minimum, in my opinion. Or vice versa: 3 in the first, 4 in the second and max. 5 in the following... And last (for today): If you consider to try progressions, don't only look at your lot-size ! You also can increase your bets by the percentage you want to risk per trade. Simple martingale example: 1 - 2 - 4 - 8 If you interpret those numers as lot size, then most of you will back off. Understandably. By looking at those numbers as UNITS you'll get another picture. If ONE unit is only 0.25 % of your account, then this sequence will read: 0.25 - 0.5 - 1 - 2 in % As you can see this in not as frightening as before. Hello guys At last i found a thread with a system that its main idea was haunting me some time now. Because i was tired of classical approaches to the markets,with easy gains at trending markets and losses at sideways, ending with no essential gains, i started trading pure priced strategy. My version of Zebulon's system: 1) Trade Eur/Jpy ONLY. Huge moves and low spread. 2)Place entry orders at xx40 and xx90 levels. That way sometimes we get away from losses because 50s/00s are psych-levels. 3) It doesnt matter the sequence. If we would start right now,and the price was at 124.65, we should put a buy entry at 124.90 and sell entry at 124.40. Stops at the SAME levels (so 50 pips win/loss every time) This way every time a trade closes another opens. 4) Martingale ONLY after three consecutive losses by doubling lots. If it wins we resume original lot size. If it losses for the fourth time(rare) the fifth position will have the same doubled position NOT quadraple like traditionally should. I prefer this conservative way. I dont think there will ever be a fifth loss(not in some backtesting i did). Its Eur/Jpy not Eur/Usd with its filthy sideways periods. If we lose again then its up to everyones risk appetite, but you cant keep this cross pair in a 100 pip range for long believe me. 5) The edge? Check the charts. A few times a month Eur/Jpy makes explosive moves. 200-400 pips in a couple of hours without looking back. What does that mean? If we are ready,with placed orders 4-5 levels below/above current price we win 2-5 trades within a few hours. 6) Every time we lose we place immediately an entry order at the previews level. Example: Price goes down and triggers sell order at 124.40. After a while it rises at 124.90 and hits our stop loss(and ofcourse it also opens our buy order) immediately we place a sell order at 124.40 again. Some backtesting for a few months give around 60% win rate BUT with our conservative version of martingale it goes higher, because the fourth martingaled trade erases one loss. Thats it guys. After so much time in forex, i can say that indicators are the devil. No indicator survives a sideways market, but price-range trading defies everything, even randomness.And the edge pseudo-trends within this chaos. And Eur/Jpy is the best of them. Please if you have suggestions post them. Xander PS: Its better 60% win rate and compounding every month, that 80% win rate through martingale
