--- 帖子 #79 ---
Who am I to answer as I'm only learning but you have a no/no for a long trade. Rules for a long, or buy trade are: 1st candle lower wick is touching or crossing lines formed by SweetSpots GOLD indicator (10 pip grid lines) while the 2nd candle upper wick is not touching or crossing the 10 pip grid lines or this candle is having no upper wick at all. I call this condition as Yes/No . That condition can work the other way around that is to say; the 1st candle lower wick is not touching or crossing the 10 pip grid lines or this candle is having no lower wick at all, while the 2nd candle upper wick is touching or crossing those lines. It is called No/Yes condition.
