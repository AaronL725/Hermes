--- 帖子 #82 ---
The arrow is always shown on already closed bar. So you should enter at the opening of a new bar, if you see the arrow on the previous one (closed). Now, the Trend_Spotter indicator DOES redraw itself, so it's normal that the arrow can appear, then disappear. That's because the indicator uses 3 bars to calculate the signal: previous, current, and FUTURE bars. But thhe future bar data cannot be known in advance, they are extrapolated! So, the indicator "thinks" that the future data will be, let' say, A, and draws the arrow. One bar later, the future bar becomes a current one, so we can get the real time data for it. Let's say this data is NOT A. Then the indicator "understands" it was wrong with the extrapolation, and the arrow disappears.

If it does, set a pending order to go short at the open price and a target at the bottom of the current bar (which you will always know because it will be 30 pips from the high).
