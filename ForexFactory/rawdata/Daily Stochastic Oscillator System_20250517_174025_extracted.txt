The SolarWind (or properly known as Fisher Tranform) commonly going rounds is a repainter. The whole idea of Fisher Transform is for smoothing (with supposedly less delay than other indicators). someone coded it wrongly to use future bars for that smothing, instead of past bars. If you want to use without re-initializing, you are basically not using Fisher Transform (still there will be small repainting bby upto 2 bars, because of the way MT4 works). If you dont use that smoothing effect, you can as well use Stochoistics crossing 50 level, both calculations become nearly same in that case!. The first link below has the repainter and the second link has non-repainter (both with alerts: pop-up, text-signal, email, sound): http://www.forexfactory.com/showpost...8&amp;postcount=10 http://www.forexfactory.com/showpost...3&amp;postcount=15 I have worked/wasted quite sometime on this codes, actually the one called Fisher/Fish (but both codes are same, SolarWind is just a fancy name for the same wrong version).
