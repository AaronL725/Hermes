Hey team- I modified Supersignal and renamed it UberfractalV02 for now. A few important notes: 1) Supersignal is really a super fractal finder. It is preset to look 12 bars to the left and 11 bars to the right (24 bars total), which is basically spotting the highest high or lowest low over a 2 hour timeframe on 5 min charts. That's also why it repaints so much, as much as an hour after you get a signal on a 5 min chart. 2) I changed the signal so you can choose how many bars to the left and right you prefer. A standard fractal, for example, would have barsToTheLeft = 2 and barsToTheRight = 2. 3) For the Paulus Protocol I suggest barsToTheLeft = 12 and barsToTheRight = 0. What you'll get is a long series of bars with signals on them with no repainting. So you're waiting for bars that change direction, don't have the signal anymore, then the iStoch signal, then go.... Yes, I know it makes for a cluttered screen. But the benefit is that it makes for more stable backtesting. After you backtest it you could then change barsToTheRight =1 or whatever for live trading.

There is a difference between retracements in a trend continuation and a trend reversal. Trend reversal occurs at the highest point. Short trade: In the case of a reversal from the highest point: price will go through the midBB and ema55 to the lowerBB. Long trade: In the case of a reversal from the lowest point: price will go through the midBB and ema55 to the upperBB. Take your profit always at the midBB (lower risk). eTrader
