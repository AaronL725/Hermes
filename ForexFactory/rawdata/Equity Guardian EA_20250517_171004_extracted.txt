Equity Guardian EA This little EA is designed to protect your account from unexpected drawdowns and/or lock in profits. Note: It does NOT place orders. It does NOT amend existing orders. It can be used alongside any other EA or manual strategy that you care to use. Features Monitors EQUITY, not simply floating profit/loss. Can monitor all trades, trades submitted by any EA or trades submitted by a specific EA. Closes all monitored trades if equity falls below a specified loss limit (expressed in either an amount or a percentage of the opening balance) Closes all monitored trades if equity reaches a specified profit target. Can disable auto trading when close is triggered. Example Information Panel Attached Image Parameters Attached Image (click to enlarge) The parameters should be self-explanatory, but for clarification: Currency pairs All pairs Current pair only Magic Number to Monitor for 0 = None : The EA will monitor ALL trades regardless of their magic number. -1 = All EA trades : The EA will monitor and control only trades submitted by an EA, i.e. trades opened manually will not be affected or included in the profit/loss calculations. &gt;0 = Only trades for the requested magic number will be monitored. Date of opening balance The date and time that EA is to calculate the initial balance from. Refresh interval How often the EA checks the equity (in seconds). Close values Percentage of balance - the profit and loss figures are a percentage of the initial balance. Amount of money - the profit and loss figures are a specific amount (in the account currency). Profit target The profit point at which the EA will be triggered (e.g. 10% profit or $100 profit, depending on the Close Basis). If set to zero, the EA will not check for a profit amount, and will never close while the account is in profit. Loss limit The loss point at which the EA will be triggered (e.g. 5% loss or $100 loss, depending on the Close Basis). If set to zero, the EA will not check for a loss amount, and will never close while the account is in loss. THIS IS NOT RECOMMENDED! Include commission? Whether or not to include commission ans swap fees when calculating the current profit amount. Close based on date/time? Whether or not to close all trades (regardless of profit or loss) at a specific date and time. Date and time to close Self-explanatory. Close pending orders? Whether or not to close pending orders when the close is triggered. Disable AutoTrading? When set to true, the EA will disable AutoTrading when triggered. Enable trailing profit? Whether or not to enable the trailing profit feature. Trailing profit trigger level The point (in either percentage of opening balance or a specific amount - see Close values parameter ) at which the trailing profit is triggered. Trail profit by The distance behind the current profit the trailing profit should be kept at. Trailing profit step The amount by which the trailing profit is to be moved each time. --- 帖子 #18 ---
The next version includes a trailing profit option. This works similar to a trailing stop, except that it keeps a track of profit, not stop levels. e.g.: Trailing profit trigger = $250 Trailing distance = $100 Trailing step = $10 In this example, when the profit hits $250, the trailing profit is triggered. The trailing profit is moved to $100 behind the current profit ($150) When the profit increases to $260 (so, +$10 - the 'step'), the trailing profit will be moved again to within $100 of the new profit ($160). If the price retraces, and the profit falls below the trailing profit, the close is triggered. You can set the profit target to zero, and use the trailing profit to lock in as much as possible.

OK, I've reviewed the trailing profit, and it looks like it's working as intended. Two things to bear in mind: The market is always moving, so the values/percentages specified will not be exact because by the time the EA reacts, the market has already moved in one direction or the other. The Step value is used to give the basket some breathing space, and when triggered, the trailing profit will be moved up, not by the Step, but by the Distance. So, a move in the trailing step may get triggered, but the market will have moved, so when the trail is moved, is may be more or less than the specified Step amount. I hope this makes sense.

Hiya, This is not how EG is intended to work. EG is intended to monitor the gain (or loss) of an account over a period of time, and to terminate all trades once a specific profit level has been reached, or a specific loss limit has been reached. This is intended to be across a number of trades, with the balance changing in the interim. The Opening balance is how it keeps a track of how much equity has been gained or lost. If the opening balance changes every time the account balance does, then there's no way to determine how much equity has been gained or lost. Whenever you change the parameters of an EA (any EA), it restarts from scratch as though you had just dropped it onto the chart. This is always a very dangerous thing to do and can result in unexpected results. However, in this instance, what happened is not unexpected. When you amended the Magic Number to Monitor parameter from (I assume) "-1" (trades opened by any EA) to "0" (all trades), you were telling it to: Calculate the starting balance as at the Date of opening balance using the current balance minus all closed trades since that date. Calculate the current equity gain or loss since that date, looking at ALL trades regardless of whether submitted manually or by EAs. It would then have compared the equity gain or loss against the Close On Equity figures you had previously entered, and if either of those values were exceeded, it would trigger an automatic close of ALL outstanding trades. So, as I said, this is exactly what I would have expected.
