--- 帖子 #30 ---
There are two Set Up Candles. One is an Up Bar/Candle the other is a Down Bar/Candle. Up Bar/Candle is defined as one where the open of the candle is in the bottom 30% of the candle and the close of the candle is in the top 30% of the candle. Down Bar/Candle is defined as the opposite. That is where the open is in the top 30% of the candle and the close of the candle is in the bottom 30% of the candle. Does that make it clearer to understand. The reasoning for using these candle as set up candles is because they are significant moves and represent direction in the market.

--- 帖子 #70 ---
Seems to be some confusion regarding the ATR and how to calculate the SL and TP levels. Use the ATR indicator and use the default period of 14. Put a hoirzontal line on the ATR Indicator and read from the Chart what the value for the ATR is. Then take this value and use this value as the initial Stop Loss Value. Initial Take Profit Value is 2/3 of the Stop Loss Value which is the ATR Value. Second Position has no Take Profit Value, only an initial stop loss Value. To begin with to make things easier just use one position and use a Take Profit Value and Stop Loss Value. As the signals become clearer then start using a second position at the same time as the initial trade and then as per the rules move the second position with the trend. See chart for explanation of how to determine Stop Loss and Take Profits. Hope this clears it up and makes it easier. Bascially ATR indicator works out the average height of each bar over the last 14 bars. Which is the average range of the high and low of each bar over the last 14 bars. This way we basically need a bar to do a complete reversal on our positon to stop us out on the initial trade. Which does not happen too often. Taking trades in this way you will achieve a success rate of around 75%-80%

--- 帖子 #78 ---
No you don't... just need to have them all pointing in the same direction. This current trade set up the moving average is just tipping down... bollinger bands are well and truly pointing down. When there is a "Trend" in place signified by the bollinger bands being above/below the 21sma then there is a setup called the retracement trade. Which is where you place an order to go long in a up trend when there is a retracement signified by a downbar. Place order to go long above the high of the down bar. As a new bar is formed then move order to go long above high of new bar. Keep doing this until get filled. Same TP and SL rules apply. Ideally retracement should be signified by a series of bars that have lower highs and lower lows for a retracment in an uptrend, and the opposite for a downtrend. If retracement moves past the 21sma then retracement trade is cancelled.
