--- 帖子 #19 ---
A trade should only enter when price is between the pink and white lines. The EA is set at = to or +3 on sells -3 on buys on the 3MA simple High/low. That is a 4 pip range. It is how I solved the spike problem of news stories so you dont get caught in a series of losing trades. As of yet I do not know if this amount of space is enough to ensure a trade gets entered or if it is too narrow. So if price has gone past the entrance point, the trade should not enter until it retraces back to the line. All entrances and exits are based on the bid line. The hopeful beauty of this then is if you see price spike up past the line, you turn the EA on hopefully catching the retracement later. This is a change from what we were testing earlier in the 4H trading thread. I had this problem with spikes in my earlier EA tests so I changed to this. If I take a SL I only want one not several. The other part of the theory is if price retraces back and enters after a stop loss you regain back most if not all of the SL very quickly. Anyway that is how it is suppose to work. If a broker or bank uses spikes to clear out stop losses we still get back in the trade at a good point and recover our loss quickly. See example pg.1 post #11 trades 2 &amp; 7 would have hit their stop loss but then retraced, entered and went on to profit. They may not have earned the total 30pip SL back but they would get most of it. See also post #12 trades 13 and 14 for the same thing. So what I tried to design in the system that if you hit a stop loss most of the time you will earn the pips back in the very next trade going with the trend. That is a deliberate design in the system. On the other hand if price moves hundreds of pips away during a 4H run. The EA will never reenter and you have until the end of the 4H bar to turn the EA off or plan another strategy. I thought this strategy would be better in the long run than using an EA that would do one trade and quit and then you would have to reset it. So that was my thinking behind the narrow entrance point.

Almost all indicators are moving averages mixed with a mathematical formula and then shown in a way other than a line on a chart. They may use bars, histograms, etc. but they are just fancy moving averages. Now when you use a 3MA high or any MA when a bar opens the line moves into a position that equals the Math being used at the time. As price moves up the MA line moves up because the new numbers of price being inputted into the math formula are getting bigger so the line goes higher with the new formula. So what is happening when a candle opens the MA line is farther down than later when the final high of that bar is mathmatically fixed for that bar. What we see is the final line and we are basing our trade decisons on that final line but before price ever reaches that high the MA line is not fixed but is moving. I also have added a 7 pip area to that line and that can cause the entrance to be much lower than the final line we see later. So lower entrances will cause more SL hits and some of our entrances will not be where we want them. Now solving that problem, hmmmmmmmmm that is going to be tough.
