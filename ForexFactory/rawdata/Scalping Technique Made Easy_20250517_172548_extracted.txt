If you have a 5M chart open with 4 multi-timeframe indicators showing 5M, 15M, 30M and 1H, the 1H bars will be the same colour for 12 consecutive bars as there are 12 5M bars for every 1H bar It is possible, therefore, that all 4 MTF indicators show red for a series of bars, for example. If there is a sudden move upwards towards the end of an hour, though, the 5M indicator will show blue. If the move is big enough, the last 3 bars of the 15M indicator will also change to blue, and also possible that the last 6 30M bars and last 12 1H bars also change to blue. So you may have been making trades based off a series of red bars, which an hour later may well have changed to a series of blue bars.
