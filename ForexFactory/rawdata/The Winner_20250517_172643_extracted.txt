OK - now to the exits. The hard part. Though if you have a good entry strategy, then the exits gets easier. It will be about how much profit to take, rather than not to lose. There are loads of ways to exit. I will just use a few here. RRW = 1:1. When you enter the trade, then set the take profit in the same distance as the stop loss. RRW = 1:x. x can be 2, 3 or whatever. You will lose more often, but will be compensated by the x. Let it run. Take as much as you can before it close. Using 1 or 2, you will leave pips on the table most times. Using 3 you will lose some gain before you finally close the trade. If you want a steady income. Use 1 or 2. That is the wisest thing to do. If you are clever in chosing the the instruments to trade - as stated in post #1 - then you will have gains most of the time. If you are a family father/mother and dayjobber, then you can set&amp;forget every day at market close. This is the way to do it for you. Just one more thing - and this is important. When we get in on green MACD and CCI on the Daily, then we can ride the trend for some time - on the M30. But if we ride the trend until one of the indicators on the Daily gets red, then we have lost some gain to the croupier. Here comes one of the genious things If you trade the M30, then have the MIKA on the Daily. The Mika will tell early when the trend is stopping. At that time stop trading the M30, because it will go ranging. As the nature of MIKA, the trend might kick back on the Daily. Then the MIKA on the Daily will tell you, that it is ok to continue trading the M30 again. So this way, we keep out of bad trades on M30. Backside is, that your runs will be cut a little too often. So in a long runnings, the EMA exit is far the best and in the short runnings, the MIKA might be better. Then again, you never know how far the runs will continue.

If the break is small, then it can be an advantage to place a stoploss below the bar. If it goes back into the trend, then you get more pips, otherwise you get out. NOTE!!!! A wick is NOT an exit signal. We are only talking close of bars. Note, the indicator can show arrows and it can be helpfull to observe them, but it is much better to observe the pullback and bounce of a trending moving average and use CCI/MACD as enter signals.
