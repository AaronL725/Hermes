The code ensures the equivalent configuration for the upper lines. Pippo is hard coded to only work on the lowest and highest 4 lines 2) Sure. --- 帖子 #94 ---
Like most people, sometimes I have a ton of charts open and different indicators smattered across them. Sometimes my system hangs and sometimes when I restart it it won't load. One way around this is to go to your profiles folder in your MT4 platform folder and look for a file called "LastProfile". To get your system to restart and prove that it is a chart, EA or indicator hanging, open the file and change the word in there to "default" or what ever the name is of one of your more simple profiles were that was working. Save and close the folder, close platform and restart. The Mt4 platform will open with this new chart set. From there you can try to load the profile you were running. If it doesn't work then you may have to rebuild all of the charts and resave the profile. Another trick I have learned is that when building up a set of charts, I will add a few charts and indicators and then save save it as Prof-1, add some more and save it as Prof-2......prof-15 etc. This way if I need to restart my system I can go back and work my way through the different profiles until I find where the problem is and then rebuild from there. These methods have saved me a lot of headache and time reloading MT4 platforms, charts, indicators and EAs.
